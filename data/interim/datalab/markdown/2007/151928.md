# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS December 4, 2007**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor McGrath called the regular December 4, 2007 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present: Mayor McGrath and Council Members Ageton, Appelbaum, Cowles, Espinoza, Gray, Morzel, Osborne, and Wilson

Those absent: None

- A. Presentation of the Janet Driskell Turner Outstanding Adult Learner Award for Literacy presented, by Dianna Sherry from the Boulder Reads Program and Mayor McGrath, to Maputo Mensah from Ghana.

## **2. PUBLIC PARTICIPATION and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE**

- 1. Dan Corson, representing the Colorado State Historical Society, noted that 2009 will be Boulder's sesquicentennial (150<sup>th</sup> anniversary) and a committee s being formed to plan a celebration. The website for the committee is [www.boulder150.com](http://www.boulder150.com) He asked council to consider appointing one or more members to participate on the committee.
- 2. Silvia Pettem presented a brief slide show of Boulder's history.
- 3. Robert Cohen spoke regarding the Foothills Park-n-Ride, and suggested that the city negotiate with CU as a swap for the Flatirons property with Open Space.
- 4. Sandra Godden spoke regarding Agenda Item 3J, and asked for a variance to again allow parking in the center of the cul-de-sacs for Apache West and East Courts in Frasier Meadows.
- 5. Steve Pomerance spoke regarding communications with council, specifically noting that CAC minutes and staff to council communication should be posted to the hotline, as they are all public documents. He also voiced concern regarding the process of the competitive bid for the sale of the Park-and-Ride property.
- 6. Mitchell Smith spoke regarding Agenda Item 3J, noting that the exception for business vehicles is flawed. He distributed a photograph of a trailer loaded with a tractor, and asked council to call up the item to address this type of situation.

{1}------------------------------------------------

7. Jim Leach, representing Wonderland Hill Development Company, spoke regarding the Washington Village project. He provided council with a letter outlining the project's history and the current petition drive to stop the project, and then requested the city's help in finding a solution.
8. Ed Byrne representing a loser of the competitive bid for the Park-and-Ride property, agreed with the comments by Mr. Pomerance, and expressed his concern that the bid did not say that rezoning was not an option until after the bids had come in thus eliminating some interested parties from moving forward in the process. Asked council to call the item up as this should be its decision.
9. Larry McKeogh, representing Boulder Aquatic Masters, spoke regarding the cancellation of Kinetics at the Boulder Reservoir. He voiced concern about a more systemic issue that may affect overall revenues towards Parks and Recreation's programs.
10. Lynn Segal requested that council review the website [www.washingtonschool.org](http://www.washingtonschool.org).
11. Elizabeth Allen urged the new council members to not be bought off by developers, noting that they should stop the trend of over development that has been evident over the past eight years.

### **City Manager Response:**

City Manager Bruno spoke to the following:

1. Regarding the Foothills Park-and-Ride, he outlined the process that led to tonight's call up opportunity. The city attorney also clarified that notice was adequate, while noting that staff added it to this agenda to ensure that council understood that it had the ability to call-up this matter.
2. Regarding Kinetics, he clarified that KBCO is the historic sponsor of this event, and the decision to cancel next year was not related to the Parks and Recreation Department. The city stands ready to continue works with them or any other special event.
3. Regarding comments from Ms. Allen, he responded that he was very disappointed to hear any assumption that the city was or had received pay-offs of any kind. He stressed that the City of Boulder is the model of integrity from the council down through the staff and while members of the public may be unhappy with some of the planning project decisions, the integrity of the process is beyond question.

### **Council Response:**

Council Member Gray spoke regarding comments by Mr. Cohen and agreed that talking with CU about property swaps is a good idea.

{2}------------------------------------------------

Mayor McGrath noted that the Sesquicentennial item is listed under Matters on this agenda.

Council Member Osborne voiced concern about the next steps in the Washington School issue. The city attorney indicated that a memorandum would be forthcoming with options that week.

Council Member Cowles inquired about the procedural aspects of the petition certification process. City Clerk Lewis reviewed that process for council.

Council Member Morzel inquired about having the CAC minutes posted to the hotline. Staff indicated that staff was preparing a response to this that would identify certain issues that may need further discussion and clarification.
