# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS August 7, 2007**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Ruzzin called the regular August 7, 2007 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Ruzzin, Deputy Mayor Ageton, and Councilmembers Bohannan, Gray, McGrath, Polk, Stoakes and Wilson.

Those absent: Andy Schultheiss

### **A. RTD Presentation on the North West Corridor**

Presentation from Nadine Lee and Chris Quinn from RTD regarding the North West Corrdior. Mr. Quinn acknowledged that many of the issues that are being brought forth will be of significant concern to the city and that RTD is just beginning to analyze these issues. He then indicated that RTD would be happy to schedule time for an in depth conversation with the Council. Mayor Ruzzin noted that October 30 has been set aside as a potential study session date.

## **2. PUBLIC PARTICIPATION and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE – 6:30 p.m.**

1. Tim Thomas spoke of the various boards he currently sits on including the Economic Vitality Board. He requested that Council make the Economic Advisory Board an on-going formal board.
2. Seth Brigham spoke to the results of the open records request documents he received dealing with the financial activity of BTV. More specifically, the amount of funds that have been used personally by BTV director Tony Perri. He requested that the city conduct an immediate audit.
3. Rob Smoke voiced his concern that a presentation was placed in front of public participation which is misleading to the viewing public. He then expressed concern regarding impacts to the Farmers Market if a Convention Center is built on 13<sup>th</sup> Street.
4. Robert Sharpe also agreed that public participation should not be bumped from the beginning of the meetings as it is disrespectful to the public. Regarding Open Space management he noted that it is seriously flawed and should be addressed through disciplinary action and firing if warranted.
5. Sylvia Pettem presented the Council with a copy of her book which records the history of Pearl Street.
6. Elizabeth Padilla voiced dismay at the lack of attention displayed by Council for the input that was coming forward. She expressed that the community does not feel engaged and offered suggestions for council to re-connect with the community (see attached hand out for generating discussion).

{1}------------------------------------------------

7. Aiden Stone, a student from Evergreen, spoke regarding a resolution he wrote concerning conservation of Color's Water Resources.
8. Nathan Knecht delivered an invitation to Council to visit the Crestview East annexation neighborhood to see first hand the challenges of preserving Upland Avenue.
9. Jim Leach with Wonderland Hill development provided an update on the Washington School project. He indicated that strong neighborhood opposition has come forward and that Wonderland Hill is making every effort to meet with the neighbors to resolve the concerns.
10. Guy Erickson stated that this Council is the worst ever and that the defense of BTV and Tony Perri are appalling. He then noted numerous allegations of embezzlement.
11. Lynn Segal suggested reinstating the Mayor and members meet and greet every month as part of social sustainability. She indicated she hoped the City Attorney would come back in five years to assist in the municipalization project.

#### **City Manager Response: - 7:06 p.m.**

City Manager Bruno responded as follows:

1. Regarding the role of the Economic Vitality Advisory Board; noted that this discussion will be part of the study session on August 14.
2. Concerning Rob Smoke and his concerns about impacts on Farmers Market by creation of a conference center; indicated a commitment to Farmers Market and even the potential of a permanent presence of the Market year round.
3. Regarding Community Dialogue comments from M. Padilla; indicated the City has traditionally conducted surveys and is now expanding that effort.
4. Affirmed that Washington School is currently going through the Planning Board process.
5. Regarding funding of BTV; provided clarity of funding agreement and oversight provided by the City. All expenditures are reviewed and if inappropriate, are rejected by the City.

Interim City Attorney Gordon noted that Washington School is currently in a quasi-judicial process which prohibits Council discussion but he indicated that the Council will have an opportunity to weigh in on the item at a later date.

#### **Council Response:**

Concerning BTV, Councilmember Polk clarified that his support for non-profits was a personal matter and that he had no intention of discontinuing this practice.

Also concerning BTV, Councilmember McGrath clarified that the accusation of embezzlement by BTV is unwarranted.

Councilmember Gray spoke to the feasibility of a convention center on 13<sup>th</sup> Street and indicated that at the recent study session it was clarified that outreach to current users to identify the potential impacts and to clarify floodplain issues would need to take place.

{2}------------------------------------------------

Mayor Ruzzin spoke to the difference between current city boards and commissions and the committees established to work on sustainability issues. He reminded the council that the sustainability committees are just getting started on their work and the role of those committees could certainly change over time. He also expressed the appreciation of the Council for the work being done by the committees.

## **5. PUBLIC HEARINGS: - 7:26 p.m.**

### **A. CONSIDERATION OF COUNCIL CHARTER COMMITTEE REPORT RELATING TO POTENTIAL CHANGES TO THE CITY OF BOULDER CHARTER WHICH MIGHT BE REFERRED TO THE PEOPLE AT THE UPCOMING NOVEMBER ELECTION. SUBJECTS SUGGESTED FOR REVIEW INCLUDE COMPENSATION FOR CITY COUNCIL MEMBERS; PROCEDURES FOR FILLING CITY COUNCIL VACANCIES; REQUIREMENTS FOR THE SIGNING OF CITY COUNCIL NOMINATION PETITIONS; TERM LIMITS FOR LEASES GRANTED BY THE CITY AND MODIFICATION OF CHARTER LANGUAGE RELATED TO APPOINTMENTS TO ADVISORY COMMISSIONS.**

Deputy Mayor Ageton introduced this item and provided the outlined process for addressing each issue.

Councilmember McGrath then reviewed the potential ordinances relating to what constitutes a vacancy and three options for how to fill a vacancy.

- Ordinance No. 7538 – Option #1; filling vacancies by elections in November and April
- Ordinance No. 7539 – Option #2; filling vacancies by Council appointment
- Ordinance No. 7540 – Option #3; filling vacancies at next feasible November election

Deputy Mayor Ageton then outlined remaining amendments including:

- Ordinance No. 7542 – Permit city leases up to thirty years
- Ordinance No. 7536 – City Council compensation
- Ordinance No. 7543 – Diversity requirements for City commissions
- Ordinance No. 7541 – City Council nomination petition requirements

{4}------------------------------------------------

The public hearing was opened:

1. Tim Thomas supported comments by Councilmember Bohannan regarding whether Board and Commission members should be required to be city of Boulder electors. He felt this issue should be considered by the Charter Committee.
2. Lisa Morzel spoke regarding Council vacancies and suggested that the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up from the last election fill the vacancies.
3. Peter Richards supported raising the compensation for Council. He then asked why the Charter Committee hadn't looked at the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up option and suggested contacting the attorney would had worked on this solution for the cities of Steamboat Springs, Aspen and Lakewood.
4. Lynn Segal also supported the idea of the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up for vacancies. She also asked that the blood alcohol level of any public official be public record if arrested and suggested integrated interests between BVSD and the City and County.

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

10:30 p.m. Deputy Mayor Ageton moved, seconded by McGrath to suspend the rules and continue the meeting. The motion carried 8:0, Schultheiss absent.

Deputy Mayor Ageton moved, seconded by Gray to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7541 submitting to the qualified electors on Tuesday, November 6, 2007 a ballot issue amending the City Charter, section 8, City Council nomination petition requirements.

Councilmember McGrath moved, seconded by Ageton to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7536 submitting to the qualified electors on Tuesday, November 6, 2007 a ballot issue amending the City Charter, section 7, concerning City Council compensation with an additional alternative to come back of \$250 per meeting with an annual cap of \$12,000 per year.

Councilmember Polk moved seconded by Wilson to direct the City Attorney to bring back an ordinance to remove the citizenship requirement for Commissions with a legal analysis.

Deputy Mayor Ageton moved, seconded by Wilson to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7542 submitting to the qualified electors on Tuesday, November 6, 2007 a ballot issue amending the City Charter, section 111, as amended to extend the maximum duration for a lease granted by the city to 40 years with a vote of two thirds of all Councilmembers in favor required for approval.

Councilmember McGrath moved, seconded by Ageton to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7537 submitting to the qualified electors on Tuesday, November 6, 2007 a ballot issue amending the first paragraph of the City Charter clarifying circumstances that cause a city council vacancy with direction to bring back an alternative to add 1st class misdemeanors.

{5}------------------------------------------------

Councilmember Gray moved, seconded by Ruzzin to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7538 submitting to the qualified electors on Tuesday, November 6, 2007 a ballot issue amending the City Charter, section 8, filling council vacancies by election in November and April as amended by the City Attorney.

Councilmember Gray moved seconded by Stoakes to direct the City Attorney to bring back two additional options for filling of council vacancies. 1) An option to appoint the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up from the most recent general election with an appointment by the Council if the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up is unable to serve; and 2) An option to appoint the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up from the most recent general election with the requirement of a special election if the 1<sup>st</sup> runner up is unable to serve.
