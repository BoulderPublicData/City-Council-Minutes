# **CITY OF BOULDER** **CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING** Municipal Building, 1777 Broadway Boulder, Colorado 80302 Tuesday, November 14, 2017

## **MINUTES**

### **1. Call to Order and Roll Call**

Mayor Jones called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m.

Council Members Brockett, Burton, Morzel, Shoemaker, Yates and Young were present with Council Member Weaver absent. Council Member Appelbaum arrived at 6:27 p.m.

#### **A. Presentation of Declarations for Council Members**

Council Member Yates presented a Declaration to Council Member Burton, Council Member Morzel presented a Declaration to Council Member Shoemaker, and Mayor Jones presented a Declaration to Council Member Appelbaum in honor of their years of service on the Boulder City Council at 6:06 p.m.

**COUNCIL MEMBER MORZEL MOVED TO APPROVE THE AMENDED AGENDA BY HAVING THE PUBLIC HEARINGS WITH THE LIQUOR OCCUPATION TAX FIRST, FOLLOWED BY THE DISCUSSION OF SEXUALLY VIOLENT PREDATORS, FOLLOWED BY BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN MAP CHANGES FOR KNOLLWOOD AND SPRING VALLEY, AND TO ADD ONE ITEM, A REQUEST FOR A NOD OF FIVE TO DIRECT STAFF TO INVESTIGATE THE POSSIBILITY OF HAVING THE CITY JOIN A LAWSUIT AGAINST LEADING OIL COMPANIES FOR COSTS INCURRED BECAUSE OF CLIMATE CHANGE. COUNCIL MEMBER YOUNG SECONDED THE MOTION. THE MOTION PASSED 7:0 WITH COUNCIL MEMBERS APPELBAUM AND WEAVER ABSENT AT 6:13 P.M.**

4. Ulla Merz

1. Lori Waldo
2. Craig Ruff

The Public Hearing closed at 8:20 p.m.

### **4. Matters from Mayor and Members of Council**

- A. Request for a “Nod of Five” to direct staff to investigate possibility of having the city join a lawsuit against leading oil companies for costs incurred because of climate change

Council Member Yates recused himself from this item at 8:57 p.m.

Mayor Jones brought this item forward as a potential lawsuit involving forward thinking climate litigation. City Attorney Carr presented this item further to Council, informing that San Francisco and Oakland have both filed lawsuits limited to the five (5) largest oil companies. City Attorney Carr indicated the “Nod of Five” would allow him to work with the group that has approached Boulder to investigate whether this is a viable lawsuit before Boulder decides whether to go forward or not. Council discussed the potential lawsuit in greater detail.

A “Nod of Five” was given by Council at 9:02 p.m.
