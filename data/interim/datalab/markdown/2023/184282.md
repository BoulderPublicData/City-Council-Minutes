### **3. Consent Agenda**

- A. Consideration of a motion to **approve the 2024 Council Meeting Calendar**
- B. Consideration of a Motion to authorize **the City Manager to enter into an Agreement with Tanoa Inc**, covering a full-service restaurant at Flatirons Golf Course
- C. Consideration of a motion to **approve a Pole Attachment Agreement with New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC for a lease term of ten years** to allow for certain small cell wireless facilities to be attached to streetlights that will be acquired from Public Service Company of Colorado in 2024
- D. Consideration of a motion to **adopt Resolution 1342 designating certain streets** within the City of Boulder **as automated vehicle identification corridors**; and setting forth related details
- E. **Introduction**, first reading, consideration of a motion to publish by title only, and **adopt as an emergency measure Ordinance 8610 an Emergency Ordinance** of the City of Boulder, Colorado (acting through its Water Utility Enterprise and its Wastewater Utility Enterprise), **authorizing the issuance of its Water and Sewer Revenue Bonds, Series 2023** for the purpose of a) providing funds to construct, acquire, improve and equip certain capital
