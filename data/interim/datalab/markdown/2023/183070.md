## **5. Matters from the Mayor and Members of Council**

- A. Discuss** a proposed **unification of ballot measures** seeking to extend the 0.15% sales tax

Council directed staff to draft an alternative 50/50 option for the measure to be presented at first reading on the June 20th meeting agenda.

Council also asked staff to research if ballot measures can be formatted to have voters pick “options” and, to reach out in the next few weeks to the organizations that may be affected by budget changes/trade-offs and

inquire how this measure would affect their operations if the funding was changed.

### **B. Discuss extending mid-term recruitment for the boards or commissions** that did not receive any applications

Elesha Johnson, City Clerk and John Morse, Elections Administrator provided Council with a background presentation and answered questions from Council on this year’s mid-term recruitment.

Council elected to not extend the mid-term recruitment period and defer recruitment for those boards or commissions to the 2024 annual recruitment.
