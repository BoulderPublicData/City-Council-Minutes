**B. Public Hearing and Concept Plan Review and Comment for proposed redevelopment at 2801 Jay Rd.** to include 84 for-sale dwelling units ranging from 1,050 SF to 1,800 SF, consisting of townhome, duplex, and triplex housing types. The plan proposes 40% (34) middle income affordable units. The percent of affordable housing community benefit will be finalized through annexation. **Reviewed under case no. LUR2022-00038.**

Shannon Moeller, Planning Manager, provided a presentation and answered questions from Council.
