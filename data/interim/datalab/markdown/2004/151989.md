### **A. SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 7351 AMENDING TITLE 9, “LAND USE REGULATION,” B.R.C. 1981, BY CHANGING THE REGULATIONS FOR FLOOR AREA RATIOS IN THE RB1-X ZONING DISTRICT TO PERMIT ADDITIONAL HOUSING WITHIN THE DOWNTOWN BY ALLOWING THE EXISTING 0.5 FLOOR AREA RATIO EXCLUSION FOR ABOVE GRADE PARKING TO ALSO BE USED FOR RESIDENTIAL USES.**

The public hearing was opened.

There being no speakers the public hearing was closed

Councilmember Eldridge moved, seconded by Riggle to adopt Ordinance No 7351 amending Title 9, “Land Use Regulation,” B R C 1981, by changing the regulations for floor area ratios in the RB1-X zoning district to permit additional housing within the downtown by allowing the existing 0.5 floor area ratio exclusion for above grade parking to also be used for residential uses. The motion carried 8.1, Gray opposed

### **B. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION RELATING TO LAKEWOOD PIPELINE TO:**

- 1. AUTHORIZE CITY STAFF TO ACCEPT THE NON-COMPLIANT PIPE, AND;**
- 2. DIRECT THE CITY ATTORNEY AND THE CITY MANAGER TO PROCEED WITH ALL STEPS NECESSARY TO STIPULATE CONDITIONS OF ACCEPTANCE INCLUDING INITIATING AND DEFENDING LITIGATION, AND INCLUDING RECOVERING MONETARY DAMAGES AND COSTS OF LITIGATION.**

The public hearing was opened.

- 1 Steve Pomerance: 335 17<sup>th</sup> St Strongly urged a veto as the pipeline was originally intended to be “internally repaired” for 100 years and now 4 miles of the pipeline is already bad. Ask the question, what if?
- 2 Ben Bender: 720 So 41<sup>st</sup> Concerned about the quality if the pipeline and feels Council should take a stand

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed

Councilmember McGrath moved, seconded by Riggle to approve #2 of this item only with the stipulation to take steps to remain consistent with recommendations of SRT with conditions coming back to Council before final acceptance. The motion carried 9-0.

### **A. UPDATE ON WEST NILE VIRUS MOSQUITO CONTROL**

Alice Guthrie gave an update regarding infectious mosquitos within the city. She noted there were none reported at this time. Reiterated that any standing water in outdoor containers (e.g. birdbaths etc.) should be exchanged every three to four days.
