# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS**

**FEBRUARY 17, 2004**

## **1     CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Toor called the regular February 17, 2004 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers

Those present were Councilmembers Bohannan, Eldridge, Gray, McGrath, Riggle, Ruzzin, Stoakes and Toor. Councilmember Schultheiss was absent

4 **PUBLIC HEARINGS:** - none

**3H. SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF EMERGENCY ORDINANCE No. 7347 EXTENDING THE PROVISIONS OF INTERIM ORDINANCE No. 7322 UNTIL AUGUST 18, 2004, BY AMENDING SECTION 6-1-12.5, “LIMITATION ON LETHAL MEANS OF CONTROL FOR PRAIRIE DOGS AND BIRDS AND REQUIREMENT FOR THE USE OF HUMANE METHODS OF RELOCATION FOR PRAIRIE DOGS,” B.R.C. 1981, AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO.**

The public hearing was opened

- 1 Lindsey Sterling, 774 Cougar, 80302, Indicated that poisoning was taking place within the city of Boulder and indicated that development is planned at Valmont Butte & the water treatment plant (both sites have numerous dogs).
- 2 Jessica Sandler, 4800 Baseline Rd, #E104-390, 80303, Again raised concerns that the current ordinance is wide open for killing prairie dogs Noted that staff had been directed to make a list of all property/owners that have prairie dogs on their property
- 3 Jill Bielawski, 280 County Rd. 83, 80302; re: Noted that although Open Space policy is not to kill prairie, it does allow them to be removed for the purpose of feeding black footed ferrets Applauded suggestion to remove language “otherwise authorized by state law ”
- 4 Pam Wanek, 2251 154<sup>th</sup> Pl , 80020; Asked if Department of Local Affairs has been contacted regarding state extermination Urged Council to use them as a resource

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed

Councilmember Ruzzin moved, seconded by Riggle to adopt Emergency Ordinance No 7347 extending the provisions of interim Ordinance No. 7322 until August 18, 2004, by amending Section 6-1-12.5, “Limitation on Lethal Means of Control for Prairie Dogs and Birds and Requirement for the use of Humane Methods of Relocation for Prairie Dogs,” B R C, 1981, and setting forth details in relation thereto. The motion carried 8-0, Schultheiss absent
