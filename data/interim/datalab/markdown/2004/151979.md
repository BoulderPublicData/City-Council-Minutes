There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Stoakes to approve the Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan changes and Action plan as amended

1. A new Service Commercial land use designation definition described below on page 6.
2. Plan Amendment Section revisions as listed in Attachment B, page 16
3. Subcommunity and Area Plan Amendment procedures revisions as listed in Attachment D, page 29
4. Economic Section revisions as listed in Attachment E, page 30
5. Master Plan and Program Summary revisions as shown in Attachment F, page 43 (Parks and Recreation Master Plan Summary, Transportation Master Plan Summary and Raw Water Master Plan Summary)

Councilmember Toor offered a friendly amendment to amend the Action Plan 7 12 page 123 - (not cross out first 4 columns) and page 125 - 2<sup>nd</sup> bullet to include a library site. The friendly amendment was accepted

1. 30th Street Neighborhood (east side of 30th between Baseline and Madison Avenue) Land use designation change from Medium Density Residential to Low Density Residential to reflect the existing development pattern and current zoning
2. South side of Madison Avenue, east of 30th Street Land use designation change from Medium and Low Density Residential to High Density Residential to reflect the existing development pattern and current zoning
3. 28th Street Frontage parcels (900 & 910 28th Street). Land use designation change from Transitional Business to High Density Residential to signal a desire for additional high density housing in this area and to implement item #3 of Resolution 922 to consider the potential for higher housing densities on parcels adjoining the University of Colorado
