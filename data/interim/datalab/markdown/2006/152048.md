# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS MUNICIPAL BUILDING, 1777 BROADWAY March 20, 2006 6:00 p.m.**

## **AGENDA**

### **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Ruzzin called the regular March 20, 2006 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00p.m.in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Councilmembers Ageton, Bohannan, Eldridge, Gray, McGrath, Polk, Ruzzin, Schultheiss and Stoakes.

### **2. PUBLIC PARTICIPATION and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE**

1. Lisa Carter, PO Box 17247, spoke to the importance of making balanced and moderate board appointments in order to create balanced boards, and that extreme positions polarize the boards.
2. Pat Shanks spoke regarding the annexation by vote. He spoke to the comprehensive plan requirements for annexations, and expressed that defining a special option, or benefit to the community requires intensive public input, the best way to determine the true desire of the community.
3. Steve Pomerance clarified several points that the group would be willing to negotiate on, with regard to the annexation initiative proposal. He also acknowledged the good work by the city staff in its memo, noting that the memo was unbiased and complete.
4. Dick Harris voiced support for citizens' votes for major annexations. He spoke of the importance of maintaining the ability to provide starter homes, but pointed out that voted annexations put a further burden on the petitioner to prove a community benefit.
5. Sue Anderson asked council to place the annexation initiative on the November ballot. The committee is very willing to work with Council to accommodate wording of the ballot issue.
6. Ruth Schrock, 860 14<sup>th</sup>, asked for continued support for 700-800 blocks of University Place Historic District.
7. Joe de Raismes, 1800 Broadway, attorney for Caplan and Earnest, spoke on behalf of the Mapleton Concerned Citizens, asking again that Council call-up the Mapleton Place/Howe Mortuary proposal.
8. Nancy Pierce, 909 Mapleton, a Mapleton Hill resident, spoke in opposition to the landmark alteration certificate for Howe Mortuary. She asked Council to call-up this discussion.
9. Gordon Reinert, 1401 Pearl Street, also spoke in favor of a call-up of the Howe Mortuary Landmark Alteration.
10. David Hose, 900 Pearl St., also spoke in support of a call-up of the Howe Mortuary Alteration. He expressed that there is an opportunity for character development that would complement the Historic District.

{1}------------------------------------------------

11. Matt McConnell, 1111 Pearl St., regarding Howe Mortuary site, encouraged Council to provide the necessary checks and balances when allegations of improper process have taken place.
12. Brett Farkas, 1019 Spruce, spoke in opposition to the How Mortuary proposal and distributed a photo that illustrates the mass and scale of this project, noting that it is inappropriate.
13. Jeff McWhitter, 5635 Illini Way, spoke regarding the proposed annexation initiative. He urged Council to place this on the ballot, noting that there are very few other decisions that are as important to the community. He also spoke to the immense level of distrust citizens have in the current process.
14. Bruce Dierking, 2595 Canyon Blvd., Ste 200, an attorney for Packard and Dierking, representing the Mapleton Place Historic Landmark Alteration Approval, asked Council to continue its support of the Landmarks Board.
15. Catherine Schweiger, 628 Maxwell, asked Council to call-up the Howe Mortuary proposal. She indicated that she did not believe the Landmarks Advisory Board understood the magnitude of this project.
16. Adrian Sopher, 3166 8<sup>th</sup> St., spoke regarding the annexation proposal. He is opposed to having the initiative placed on the ballot. He expressed that this initiative does not display good public policy, and that marketing campaigns suddenly will drive annexations rather than good planning.
17. Peter Richards, 470 University, spoke in support of placing the annexation initiative on the November ballot.
18. Dave Secunda, 1945 Grove, spoke regarding the Open Space and Mountain Parks commercial use, and voiced support of the implementation.

#### **City Manager Response:** - 7:00 p.m.

City Manager Bruno noted that items addressed during public participation would be addressed during the meeting.

#### **Council Response:** - 7:00 p.m.

Councilmember Gray clarified that Council's action regarding the annexation initiative at this meeting would be whether or not to direct staff to come back to council with an ordinance for consideration placing the initiative on the November ballot.

### 10. **FINAL DECISIONS ON MATTERS:** Action on motions made under Matters.

Vote was taken on the motion to call-up the landmark alteration certificate for 2121 11<sup>th</sup> Street, Howe Mortuary. The motion failed, 2:6, Ageton, Bohannan, McGrath, Polk, Ruzzin, and Stoakes opposed, Eldridge recused.

The motion failed, 3:6, Ageton, Bohannan, Eldridge, Polk, Ruzzin and Stoakes opposed.
