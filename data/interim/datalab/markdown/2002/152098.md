# **CITY OF BOULDER CITY COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS**

**February 5, 2002**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Will Toor called the regular February 5, 2002 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Havlick, Mock, Morzel, Poinsatte, Riggie and Ruzzin.

Prior to opening Citizen Participation, Mr. Don Chapman presented the Mayor and Councilmembers, City Manager Secrist, Fire Chief Donner and Deputy Fire Chief Stoltz, with certificates of appreciation from Sister City Manté, Mexico for the donation of a Fire Truck. Deputy Mayor Eldridge and Deputy Fire Chief Stoltz will be traveling to Manté this week to attend the dedication of the truck.

### **Staff Response:**

City Manager Secrist responded as follows:

1. Regarding EEO comments - noted that on February 2<sup>nd</sup> at 6:00 pm a Study Session will be held on Code Enforcement Issues. A zoning history will be provided as part of that Session.
2. E-Bikes - This issue has gone before two Boards but more information and research needs to take place prior to coming to Council.
3. Clarified that 10% subsidy of housing is not a Council goal, but rather to have 10% of the residential dwellings deed restricted at an affordable rate. Taxes would still be paid by the owner.
4. Reemphasized Transportation Goals - regionalism - transit connections and TDM focus which would include the possibility of car and van pools.

## **4. MATTERS FROM THE CITY MANAGER:**

### **A. UPDATE ON JOBS TO POPULATION BALANCE TASK FORCE - 7:20 P.M.**

Planning Director Peter Pollock indicated that with regard to projections, progress had been made (with assistance of a panel of participants) in two areas:

1. The land use scenario of 1.5% growth between now and 2020 has been verified by DRCOG (Denver Regional Council of Governments), the Ross Consulting Group and the Business Research Center at C.U. They also agreed with the assumptions used in the scenario.
2. Panelists also helped identify the type of growth and future development as: 1) not retail - will remain constant; 2) Manufacturing will decrease; and 3) A likely increase in high technology and health care development.

Focus will continue to be on 1) where are we now, 2) where will we be in 2020 and 3) what does our current zoning allow? A primary question for the joint study session on February 28<sup>th</sup> will be “what are our objectives?”

### **DISCUSSION ITEMS:**

## **9. DISCUSSION ITEM RELATING TO THE BEVERAGE LICENSING AUTHORITY. - 8:05 P.M.**

Discussion ensued regarding whether Council had an interest in looking further at the possibility of transforming the Beverage Licensing Board from a 5 member citizen board to a Hearing Officer (municipal judge).

After discussion, Council indicated through a straw poll that it would be interested in bringing forward an ordinance for consideration and public input but would like that to occur in April so as not to negatively impact the recruitment process currently underway for Board and Commission applicants. Council also asked staff to provide further analysis regarding historic numbers regarding vacancies and number of applicants annually, reason for recent resignations, what other communities do, are there potential improvements that can be made to the current structure without changing the make up of the board and does the Colorado Municipal League have a position/recommendation with regard to this issue.
