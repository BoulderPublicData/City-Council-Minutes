# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS June 4, 2002**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Will Toor called the regular June 4, 2002 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers, Eldridge, Havlick, Mock, Morzel, Poinsatte, Riggle and Ruzzin. Councilmember Corson was absent.

## **4. MATTERS FROM THE CITY MANAGER:**

### **A. UPDATE ON THE INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT PROGRAM - 7:05p.m.**

Deputy City Manager Christine Andersen updated City Council on the status of the city’s Integrated Pest management Program, progress made on the recommendations of the Environmental Management Audit, April 2001, and discussed the outcome of the May 20, 2002, Parks and Recreation Advisory Board discussion of the City Council pesticide moratorium.

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Havlick, to: 1) support continuation of the IPM program for parks; 2) Recommend a study session in August or September to review the draft IPM plan for Parks, Best Management Practices and Maintenance Standards; and 3) Endorsing a continuation of the City Council moratorium on ground spraying in Civic enter Park ad the 50 ft. buffer around waterways until the study session in August or September and expanding the moratorium to include aerial spraying of trees in Civic Center park and within the waterway buffer, except with EPA Toxicity Category IV compounds.

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Riggle, to amend the motion to allow treatment in the creek corridors with established IPM criteria for pest management of noxious weeds.

8:55p.m. - Peter Pollock provided an update regarding the 1041 review with the University of Colorado and Boulder County regarding the proposed tennis court at the Flatirons property.

Vote was taken on the motion to: 1) support continuation of the IPM program for parks; 2) Recommend a study session in August or September to review the draft IPM plan for Parks, Best Management Practices and Maintenance Standards; and 3) Endorsing a continuation of the City Council moratorium on ground spraying in Civic enter Park ad the 50 ft. buffer around waterways until the study session in August or September and expanding the moratorium to include aerial spraying of trees in Civic Center park and within the waterway buffer, except with EPA Toxicity Category IV compounds as amended to allow treatment in the creek corridors with established IPM criteria for pest management of noxious weeds. ## **PUBLIC HEARINGS:**

9. **SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF EMERGENCY ORDINANCE No. 7215 AMENDING SECTIONS 11-1-49, “WATER CONSERVATION MEASURES,” 11-1-50, “SPECIAL PERMITS,” AND 11-1-51, “ENFORCEMENT,” B.R.C. 1981, TO SET FORTH MEASURES FOR WATER CONSERVATION PURPOSES, INCLUDING RESTRICTIONS ON WATER USE, AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO. - 10:10P.M.**

{7}------------------------------------------------

The public hearing was opened:

1. Rob Smoke, 2445 19<sup>th</sup>; felt that penalizing city staff may not be appropriate approach - also expressed concern about not supporting adequate watering for vegetable gardens.
2. Lynn Segal, 538 Dewey; supported watering once a week for 30 minutes rather than twice a week for 15 minutes.
3. Catherine Schweiger, 628 Maxwell; as an expert landscape manager, urged that 30 minutes once a week or 15 minutes twice a week - also expressed desire to provide water for vegetables rather than swimming pools.
4. Barry Sidwell, 4360 Hanover; also urged more flexibility - suggested a gallon consumption figure rather than daily restrictions.
5. Ramona Clark, 45 Cresman; supported comments from speaker 3 and 4.

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Mayor Toor moved, seconded by Eldridge to adopt Emergency Ordinance No. 7215 amending Sections 11-1-49, "Water Conservation Measures," 11-1-50, "Special Permits," and 11-1-51, "Enforcement," B.R.C. 1981, to set forth measures for water conservation purposes, including restrictions on water use, and setting forth details in relation thereto as amended by staff. The motion carried 7:0 as amended; Corson and Morzel absent.

Councilmember Poinsatte moved, seconded by Havlick to direct staff to investigate a possible ordinance regarding water block rate increases and bring back for the July 9, 2002 Council meeting. The motion carried 6:1, Corson and Morzel absent.

Mayor Toor moved, seconded by Mock to suspend the rules and continue the Council Meeting. The motion carried 6:1, Corson and Morzel absent.
