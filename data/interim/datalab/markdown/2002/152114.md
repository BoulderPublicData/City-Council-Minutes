# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS October 22, 2002**

### **5:00 p.m. - Joint Dinner with the Boulder Housing Authority**

The Boulder Housing Partners met with the City Council to review the management side of the organization, and the acquisition and development side of the organization. In addition the Housing Partners outlined the strategic plan and commitments to the community.

## **SPECIAL MEETING**

### **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Toor called the special October 22, 2002 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:15 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Havlick, Morzel, Poinsatte, Riggle and Ruzzin. Councilmember Mock was absent.

### **2. MATTERS FROM MAYOR AND MEMBERS OF COUNCIL:**

#### **A. ITEMS RELATED TO THE CITY MANAGER SELECTION PROCESS:**

##### **1. NOMINATIONS OF CANDIDATES TO INVITE TO INTERVIEW FOR THE CITY MANAGER POSITION.**

After discussion the following motion was made:

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Morzel, to invite 6 candidates to interview with a 1<sup>st</sup> alternate identified. The motion carried: 5:3, Corson, Eldridge and Havlick opposed, Mock absent.

Council identified that candidates A, H, D, I, and E would be invited. However, there was a deadlock between candidates C and J.

Councilmember Havlick moved, seconded by Morzel, to continue the October 22<sup>nd</sup> special meeting until 4:45 p.m. on October 29<sup>th</sup>. The motion carried: 8:0, Mock absent.

{1}------------------------------------------------

##### **2. DISCUSSION OF MATTERS RELATED TO THE CITY MANAGER SELECTION PROCESS. - 8:00 P.M.**

Regarding interview process, the Council recommended using a similar process to that used in 1999, with the exception of having a candidate dinner with the consultant on Thursday evening. Council also indicated a desire to have the reception on Friday begin earlier so as not to create a hardship the early interviewee's on Saturday morning. Also, given the time of year, the tour should be scheduled at an earlier time of day.

Regarding the contract, Council consensus was to approve the draft contract. Discussion took place regarding the 30 day resignation notice, however and Council urged recommended remaining flexible on this point.

Discussion of the various options for compensation plans provided by the Mercer Group resulted in Council recommending that the current process remain in place in terms of moving forward with the job offer, however, Council stated an interest in receiving feedback on the preference of the candidates. It was also noted that the current bonus program should be evaluated on a regular basis to determine if an adjustment to base is warranted periodically to insure that the salary range remains within the market range.

#### **B. 3<sup>RD</sup> QUARTER COUNCIL GOAL REPORTS - 9:00P.M.**

The goal reports were presented in the packet. There were no questions or comments from Council.

### **ADJOURNMENT**

There being no further business to come before Council at this time, BY MOTION REGULARLY ADOPTED, THE MEETING WAS ADJOURNED AT 9:00 P.M.
