# **CITY OF BOULDER** **CITY COUNCIL JOINT SPECIAL MEETING WITH PLANNING BOARD** Municipal Building, 1777 Broadway Boulder, Colorado 80302 Thursday, October 13, 2016

## **MINUTES**

### **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Jones called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m.

Council Members Appelbaum, Brockett, Morzel, Shoemaker, Weaver, Yates and Young and Planning Board Members Gerstle, Gray, May, Payton, Putnam, and Zuckerman were present. Planning Board Member Bowen was absent.

Deputy City Attorney David Gehr outlined procedures for the public hearings.

### **2. PUBLIC HEARING**

Joint Public Hearing with Planning Board to address public request for changes to the BVCP land use map for four properties within city limits (Area I)

#### **A. Naropa University**

Planning, Housing and Sustainability Senior Planner Jay Sugnet introduced this item to Council and Planning Board Members 6:16 p.m. Deputy City Attorney David Gehr and Deputy Director of Planning, Housing and Sustainability Susan Richstone answered questions from Council and Planning Board Members.

Kristen Uitto, Managing Architect, Caddis Architects, on behalf of Requestor, spoke in support and answered questions from Council and Planning Board members.

The public hearing opened at 7:02 p.m. and the following spoke:

- i. 2103 Arapahoe – no speakers
- ii. 6287 Arapahoe – no speakers

The public hearing closed at 7:02 p.m.

#### **B. 385 Broadway**

Planning, Housing and Sustainability Senior Planner Jay Sugnet introduced this item to Council and Planning Board Members at 7:03 p.m. and answered questions from Council and Planning Board Members.
