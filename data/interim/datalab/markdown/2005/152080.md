# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS**

**May 17, 2005**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Ruzzin called the regular May 17, 2005 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Ruzzin and Councilmembers, Ageton, Bohannan, Gray, McGrath, Riggle, Schultheiss and Stoakes. Councilmember Eldridge was absent

- A. Councilmember Gray presented the zero waste Community Partnership Award for 2005 which was awarded to the city by the Eco-cycle.

## 5. **PUBLIC HEARINGS:**

### **A. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO PROVIDE DIRECTION ON DEVELOPMENT OF AN URBAN WILDLIFE MANAGEMENT PLAN AND ENDORSE THE PUBLIC PROCESS AND TIMELINE. – 7:05PM**

Staff presentation by Bev Johnson.

The public hearing was opened:

1. Mary Louise Chavers, PO Box 4342, 80306; Please do not decrease the public process. Shared a poem that reflected on the connectedness between humans and animals.
2. Billie Gutgoell, 340 So. 42, 80305; Sierra Club Indian Peaks Member – noted that the Sierra Club endorses extensive public process. Noted the challenge between balanced wild habitat and development.
3. Leslie Kinder, 428 Utica Ave., 80304; representing Friends of the Foothills – submitted a 7 page document proposing numerous recommendations (see attachment).

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Councilmember Ruzzin moved, seconded by Stoakes to delay consideration of the Urban Wildlife Plan and fold it into the 2006/2007 Budget. The motion failed 2:6; Ruzzin and Stoakes in favor, Eldridge absent.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Bohannan to approve the staff recommendation option 1 on page 5A-7 asking the Manager to return with possible Budget trade-offs or a recommendation for a supplemental appropriation. The motion carried 6:2; Ruzzin and Schultheiss opposed, Eldridge absent.

### **B. DISCUSSION AND DIRECTION ON THE 2005 WEST NILE VIRUS CONTROL PLAN. – 9:05PM**

Staff presentation by Alice Guthrie of the Environmental Affairs office.

The public hearing was opened:

There being no speakers the public hearing was closed.

Public Participation was re-opened at 9:25p.m.

1. Nolan Rosall, 4940 Pearl East Circle; DMC member asking Council support to form a task force to look for other methods to deal with the behavior issues present on the Pearl St. Mall.

5. Tim Plass, 655 Maxwell Ave., 80304; speaking as a representative of the LPAB. Spoke to two suggestions from the Chamber of Commerce that would dismantle a large portion of Boulder’s model historic preservation program. 2 Chamber proposals 1) eliminate designation of historic districts and 2) limiting landmarking to willing owners to participate in historic preservation.
6. John Salvaggio, 1397 Pearl St, 80302; victim of violent behavior on Pearl St. Mall. Spoke of the unacceptable behaviors that are taking place on a daily basis on Pearl Street Mall. Asked for a stronger police presence on the Mall.
7. Dave Allured, 4231 Eaton Ct; Boulder Bicycle commuter re: Baseline/36 project. Particularly opposed to widening on 36 on ramp. Negatively impacts bicyclists and pedestrians. Asked for on street bike lane for the entire area.
8. Rob Smoke, 2445 19<sup>th</sup> St, 80304; shared concerns about behaviors on Pearl Street Mall yet also concerned that we don’t become elitists – let’s not have unintended consequences. Also concerned about Baseline project.
