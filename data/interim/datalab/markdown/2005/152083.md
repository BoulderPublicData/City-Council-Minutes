# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS November 15, 2005 10:00a.m.**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Ruzzin called the special November 15, 2005 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 10:00a.m. in the Council Chambers. Council elects were asked to remain in the audience until the canvassing of the election was completed.

Those present were: Councilmembers Ageton, Bohannan, Eldridge, Gray, McGrath, Riggle, Ruzzin, Schultheiss and Stoakes.

### **A. ITEMS RELATING TO THE CERTIFICATION OF THE GENERAL COORDINATED MAIL BALLOT ELECTION HELD ON NOVEMBER 1, 2005:**

1. Motion to adjourn from the Boulder City Council and convene as the General Canvassing and Election Board for the City of Boulder General Coordinated Municipal Election.  
Seeing no objection, the Boulder City Council adjourned and the General Canvassing and Election Board convened.
2. Roll Call of the General Canvassing and Election Board.  
Those present were: Bohannan, Gray, Riggle, Ruzzin and Stoakes.
3. Administration of oath and signing of same by Board members.  
Oath was administered by Alisa Lewis, secretary to the General Canvassing and Election Board.
4. Consideration of a motion nominating a member to chair the Board.  
Mark Ruzzin was appointed as Chair by acclamation.
5. Submission to the Board by the Secretary of the following:
  - i. Certificate of the official ballot.
  - ii. Certificates of the official Public Notices of the November 1, 2005 Election.
  - iii. Certificate of election returns.

Alisa Lewis submitted the above documents to the Board and read for the record the official results as follows:

## 6. Public Hearing

The public hearing was opened:  
There being no speakers the public hearing was closed.

## 7. Consideration of a motion that the abstract and result sheets be approved, and signing of same if approved.

Board member Gray moved, seconded by Stoakes to approve the abstract and result sheets of the November 1, 2005 General Municipal Election for the City of Boulder. The motion carried 5:0.

## 8. Motion to adjourn from the General Canvassing and Election Board for the General Coordinated Municipal Election and reconvene as the Boulder City Council.

Seeing no objection, the General Canvassing and Election Board for the General Municipal Election adjourned and the City Council re-convened.

### 9. Swearing in of the new Councilmembers.

City Clerk Alisa Lewis administered the Oath to new councilmembersr Ageton, Eldridge, McGrath, Polk, and Schultheiss. It was clarified that Ageton, Eldridge, McGrath and Schultheiss received 4 year terms and Polk received a 2 year term.

### **B. Election of Mayor and Deputy Mayor**

Nominations for Mayor were opened:

Councilmember Stoakes nominated Mark Ruzzin for Mayor.

{2}------------------------------------------------

Hearing no objection, nominations were closed.

Mayor Ruzzin was appointed Mayor by acclamation.

Nominations for Deputy Mayor were opened:

Councilmember Schultheiss nominated Robin Bohannan.

Councilmember Eldridge nominated Suzy Ageton.

Hearing no objection, nominations were closed.

Councilmember Ageton was appointed Deputy Mayor with 5 votes by Ageton, Eldridge, Stoakes, Polk and Ruzzin.

Mayor Ruzzin gave special thanks to Tom Eldridge for serving as Deputy Mayor.

Former Councilmember Riggle addressed the Council indicting that he is teaching the President's Leadership Course at the University of Colorado to train future leader the the State, Federal and International level. He indicated that the PLC scholars must hold internship positions in order to complete the class and suggested that this provides a unique opportunity for Councilmember to serve and mentors to PC scholars and perhaps receive some wonderful support on special projects. Mr Riggle informed the Council that he would send an email with details in the next couple of days.

## **2. ADJOURNMENT**

There being no further business to come before Council at this time, BY MOTION REGULARLY ADOPTED, THE MEETING WAS ADJOURNED AT 10:22A.M.
