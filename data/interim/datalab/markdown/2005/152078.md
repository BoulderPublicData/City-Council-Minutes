### **A. REPORT FROM THE PEG ACCESS TELEVISION STUDY GROUP ABOUT CONTINUED OPERATION OF CHANNEL 54. – 7:30PM**

Anne Tapp with the PEG Access Television Study Group provided a report from the committee. Enhanced status position; important to the community, education and availability.

The public hearing was opened:

1. Len Haule, 590 Crescent Lake, 80403; has provided coverage of running events/races in Boulder. Noted that the volunteers who are doing local productions often have to supply their own tapes etc...
2. Jann Scott, PO Box 869, 80306; addressing the numbers; distributed save channel 54 stickers and a copy of the 2004 Channel 8 budget indicated that funding should continue.
3. Bill Flinchbaugh, PO Box 4133, 80306; spoke of the important services being provided to the community by CATV. Urged Council to continue funding for this important service.
4. Rob Smoke, 2445 19<sup>th</sup> Street; continue funding to whatever level possible. Inquired where the pass along funding went. Suggested that some city jobs should be cut by 40%.
5. Peter Gowen, 5494 Raritan Place, 80303; CATB Board member – supported the study group proposal.
6. Brett Parnes, 4825 Thunderbird Cir, 80303; spoke in support of returning the PEG funds to CATV to the extent possible. Also spoke in support of the consolidation of Channel 8 and 54.
7. Guy Errickson, 4705 Harrison Ave, 80303; Expressed that if 54 was being run well the amount of time and research wouldn't be taking place. He indicated that Public Education was never provided even when funded at over \$300,000. Urged Council to cease funding altogether.
8. Donna Marek, PO Box 7181, 80306; Does not support current model of Public Access as it is not providing a service but rather functions as a private club.
9. Dan Culberson, 6359 Old Stage Road, 80302; As one of the original CATV producers, indicated that if the original funding mechanism was restored this discussion wouldn't be needed.
10. Nathaniel Kramer, 1035 Grandview, 80302; A resident who was welcomed by CATV – urged support.
11. Howard Zaemba, 959 Marine Street; Newcomer who was surprised by lack of support/funding and urged return of PEG funds to CATV.
12. Aaron Smith, 1196 Washburn Ct; User of CATV, indicated that he has utilized services provided by Channel 54 – urged support and noted access by High School students especially valuable.

{4}------------------------------------------------

13. Eron Brennan, 1245 Norwood; spoke in support of Public Access television – give public access same funding as Government.

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Council concurred that the study group and CATV would need to present a different vision/model no later than August in order for Council to consider changing from a zero budge approach. The Status Quo at CATV is not an option.

### **B. THIRD READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE No. 7416 AMENDING OCCUPANCY REGULATIONS BY AMENDING TITLE 12, “HUMAN RIGHTS” WITH THE ADDITION OF A NEW CHAPTER RELATED TO THE CREATION OF A DOMESTIC PARTNERSHIP REGISTRY, AMENDING THE DEFINITIONS RELATED TO A FAMILY THROUGHOUT THE BOULDER REVISED CODE 1981, MAKING CLARIFICATIONS RELATED TO OWNER OCCUPANCY REQUIREMENTS, AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO. – 9:10PM**

The public hearing was opened:

1. Steve Clason, 3305 Eastman, 80305; on behalf of Martin Acres supports ACLU.
2. Barry Satlow, 1951 Vista Dr., 80304; Representing ACLU – supports current proposal (see attachment).
3. Neil Fishman, 2135King Ave, 80302; Opposed current proposal and supports the staff and HRC proposal which allows any domestic partnership rights for occupancy.
4. Terry Cipoletti, 4794 Jackson Cir, 80303; Board President of Boulder Pride. Pride has taken a position to support full equality for all people – open it to mixed couples.
5. Michael Mills, 4020 Evans Dr., 80303; As a same sex partner – strongly opposed to the amendment limiting occupancy rights to same sex couples.
6. Lisa Spalding, 1260 Cascade, 80302; representing University Hill Neighborhood Association supports current ordinance limiting occupancy rights to same sex couples only.
7. Jill McFadden, 4289 Monroe Dr., 80303; HRC member speaking as an individual. Supports opening the ordinance occupancy privilege to all domestic partners – do not limit to same sex couples.
8. Matt Patrick, 4794 Jackson Cir, 80303; clarified that this issue came to the HRC in 2002 as a Gay and Lesbian issue – not an occupancy issue as stated by Ms. Spalding. Echoed concerns stated by Jill McFadden regarding concerns of action when gay marriage is likely to be before the state legislature in 2006.

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Councilmember Eldridge moved, seconded by Stoakes to adopt Ordinance No. 7416.

{5}------------------------------------------------

Councilmember McGrath moved, seconded by Gray to amend the ordinance to strike “not eligible to marry under state law” thus supporting the original language. The motion carried 7:2; Eldridge and Riggle opposed.

Councilmember McGrath moved, seconded by Ruzzin to amend the ordinance to limit private registry to same sex couples for the purposes of occupancy in section 12-4-6 of this ordinance. The motion failed 1:8, McGrath in favor.

Vote was taken on the main motion to adopt Ordinance No. 7416 as amended with fourth reading on the consent agenda. The motion carried 9:0.
