Staff presentation by Peter Pollock.

A group of Native Americans were invited to speak. Those who spoke were: Nelson White, Richard White Antelope and Anthony Addison.

The public hearing was opened:

1. Ben Sherman, PO Box 288, Louisville, 80027; (Pooled time with Layla Lindsey and Corrine Lindsey). Oglalla Lakota Sioux (powerpoint) Explained sacred nature of Valmont Butte. Proposes American Indian Cultural Center on site instead.
2. Carol Affleck, PO Box 534, Niwot, 80544; Pooled time with B. Affleck and Doris Debacker. Rural Historic Valmont. Butte is sacred to Native Americans and pioneers. Make historic mill a landmark and museum site, nice partner to Indian Cultural Center. Preserve Valmont Butte.
3. Cindy Mitchell, 6029 Indian Rd.; (Pooled time with Kimberly Mitchell and Ty Melton.) Butte is County landmark and deserves and requires protection. CEAP is flawed, predisposed bias shows. Report is not complete. Please preserve Valmont Butte.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Stoakes that the city not pursue a Biosolids Recycling Center. Staff should monitor the technology, economics and alternative models for accomplishing composting. The motion carried 8:1; Ruzzin opposed.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Gray to pursue construction of a Fire Training Center, the location to be decided by the Council following a re-evaluation of potential sites and development of a funding plan. The site re-evaluation and funding plan will be completed and recommendations provided to Council by October 18, 2005. The motion carried 6:3; Eldridge, Ruzzin and Schultheiss opposed.
