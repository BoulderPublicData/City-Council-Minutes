# **CITY OF BOULDER CITY COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS**

**APRIL 3, 2001**

## 1. **CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL** - 6:00 p.m.

Mayor Toor called the regular April 3, 2001 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Havlick, Lopez, Mock, Morzel, Poinsatte and Riggle.

## 2. **CITIZEN PARTICIPATION** - 6:00 p.m.

1. Dr. Michael Kennedy, P.O. Box 21122 - re: 50 year resident who was appalled by the lewd language and graphic behavior being purported on Public Access T.V. Channel 54. Urged Council to taking action against such programing.
2. Brian Hanley, 1030 Adams Circle, Apt 118 - re: furniture and nuisance party ordinances. Opposed to proposed ordinances as he felt they set a dangerous precedent for a problem that only exists in one area of the city.
3. Rob Smoke, 2445 14<sup>th</sup> St., Apt. B - re: Vinyl Slipcovers. Opposed to prohibiting outdoor couches. Also spoke in opposition to a Super Target at Crossroads.
4. Terry Rodrigue, 1076 Lincoln Place - re: Spoke on behalf of University Hill neighborhood renovation - re: Aloha Trash program- spoke in support, noting that this will be a critical element in cleaning up "the hill."
5. Eleanor DePuy, 1509 Cascade- re: also a University Hill resident speaking in favor of the Aloha program (attachment).
6. Doug Crigler, 2137 Grove- re: Goss-Grove Neighborhood Association. Spoke on behalf of association in support of the nuisance ordinance and in very strong support of the sofa ordinance.
7. Kenneth Wilson, 970 11<sup>th</sup> St. - re: Also a member of the University Hill Neighborhood Association - applauded police enforcement efforts and supports both the sofa ordinance and the nuisance party ordinance.
8. Bob McFarland, 2300 Kalmia - re: Pearl Street Mall 1) Don't mess with the sales tax and 2) keep the grass on the mall.
9. Lynn Segal, 538 Dewey- commented on the guest opinion editorial by Steve Fisher. Spoke in support of his suggestions. Also opposed to the sofa ordinance and Rocky Flats designation as a wildlife refuge (should be treated as a toxic waste site).
10. Edward Riedel, 150 South 31<sup>st</sup> St. - re: Boulder Community Velodrome. Encouraged that a letter of interest be issued to allow process to move forward.
11. Stephen Pomerance, 335 17<sup>th</sup>- re: concerns with the Comp Plan update process.
12. Jeep Campbell, 4395 Grinnell - re: couches and nuisance ordinances. Spoke in

- favor of empowering neighborhoods to protect quality of life.
13. Ruth Blackmore, 705 South 41<sup>st</sup> St. - re: Taggart Phase A report on South Boulder Creek Floodplain. The study is flawed and should not move on to Phase B. Announced that the County Commissioners rejected all options in the Phase A Taggart Report at today's hearing.
14. Crystal Gray, 1709 Spruce - presented a slide show on the Open Space purchases currently listed on the Consent Agenda.
15. Ron Forthofer, 3912 Divot Ct. - re: spoke in opposition to removing any trees or grass on the Pearl Street Mall.

### **STAFF RESPONSE** - 6:45 p.m.

City Manager, Ron Secrist responded:

- 1) Regarding CATV, the contract with this group prohibits programming interference-suggests that the issue should be raised with the CATV Board.
- 2) Clarified that the second reading and public hearing on the sofa and nuisance party ordinances is scheduled for April 17<sup>th</sup>.
- 3) Pearl Street Mall improvements - Noted that Council will be informed as plans progress. Mr. Secrist acknowledged that numerous challenges exist.
- 4) Announced that the Valmont Park Study Session on May 8<sup>th</sup> would provide opportunity to address the Velodrome issues.

City Attorney, Joe DeRaismes responded:

- 1) Distributed the CATV contract which verified the Manager's response.
- 2) Clarified that the proposed sofa ordinance prohibits burning of sofas in public property. There is already a city ordinance which bans open burning on private property.

## **ORDER OF BUSINESS**

### **FIRST READINGS**

- 9. **INTRODUCTION, FIRST READING AND CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO ORDER PUBLISHED BY TITLE ONLY AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE ISSUANCE OF BOULDER MUNICIPAL PROPERTY AUTHORITY LEASE PURCHASE REVENUE NOTE, SERIES 2001B, IN THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF \$580,000.00, AND APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING A LEASE PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR THE PURCHASE OF APPROXIMATELY 39 ACRES IN FEE TOGETHER WITH ALL MINERAL RIGHTS AND 30 SHARES OF LEFT HAND DITCH COMPANY WATER, HESTER RESERVOIR AND COUGHLIN DITCH LOCATED NORTH OF NEBO ROAD BETWEEN FOOTHILLS HIGHWAY AND NORTH 39<sup>TH</sup> STREET FROM THE HESTER FAMILY TRUST FOR OPEN SPACE AND MOUNTAIN PARKS' PURPOSES.** - 8:04 p.m.

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Morzel, to adopt and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7124 approving and authorizing the issuance of Boulder Municipal Property Authority lease Purchase Revenue Note, Series 2001B, in the aggregate principal amount of \$580,000.00, and approving and authorizing a Lease Purchase Agreement for the purchase of approximately 39 acres in fee together with all mineral rights and 30 shares of left hand Ditch Company water, Hester Reservoir and Coughlin Ditch located north of Nebo Road between Foothills Highway and North 39<sup>th</sup> Street from the Hester Family Trust for Open Space and Mountain Parks' purposes. The motion carried:9-0.

- 10. **INTRODUCTION, FIRST READING, AND CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO ORDER PUBLISHED BY TITLE ONLY TWO ORDINANCES:**

Councilmember Havlick moved, seconded by Corson, to amend, adopt and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7125 concerning the prohibition of the use of indoor furniture in an outdoor location. The motion carried: 9-0.

2. **ADOPTING A NEW SECTION 5-3-11, B.R.C. 1981, CONCERNING NUISANCE PARTIES.** 8:05 p.m.

- 11. **INTRODUCTION, FIRST READING AND CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO ORDER PUBLISHED BY TITLE ONLY AN EMERGENCY AMENDMENT TO EXISTING ORDINANCE #7032 CONCERNING THE MODIFICATION OF THE WATER AND SANITARY SEWER SERVICE ORDINANCE WITH THE BOULDER VALLEY SCHOOL DISTRICT SETTING FORTH THE PROVISION OF SUCH SERVICES TO THE NEW ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION FACILITY BEING CONSTRUCTED AT SOMBRERO MARSH, 6500 E. ARAPAHOE AVENUE. 8:42 p.m.**

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Eldridge, to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7127 an Emergency Amendment to existing Ordinance #7032 concerning the modification of the water and sanitary sewer service ordinance with the Boulder Valley School District setting forth the provision of such services to the new environmental education facility being constructed at Sombrero Marsh, 6500 E. Arapahoe Avenue. The motion carried: 9-0.
