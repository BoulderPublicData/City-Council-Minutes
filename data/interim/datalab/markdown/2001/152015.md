# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS**

**July 17, 2001**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL:**

Mayor Toor called the regular July 17, 2001 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Havlick, Poinsatte and Riggle. Councilmembers Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel were absent.

## **2. CITIZEN PARTICIPATION and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE: - 6:00 P.M.**

Prior to opening Citizen Participation, Mayor Toor called for a moment of silence to honor Mr. John Sherwood, an active community advocate, who passed away on Monday, July 16, 2001.

1. Lynn Segal, 538 Dewey–Re: voiced appreciation for the contributions by John Sherwood; and with regards to Crossroads, questioned the need for Urban Renewal Authorities and urged dis-banning of BURA.
2. Rob Smoke, 2445 19<sup>th</sup> St., Apt. 13–Re: Also acknowledged the value of John Sherwood and his numerous contributions to the Boulder community, noting that he will be missed by many.

#### **B. APPOINTMENT TO THE ARTS COMMISSION. - 6:15 P.M.**

Mayor Toor opened the nominations:

Seeing no objection the nominations were closed.

### **9. ITEMS RELATING TO THE BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (BVCP):**

### **A. CONSIDERATION OF ADDITIONAL PROPOSED CHANGES TO THE BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (BVCP) POLICIES INCLUDING THE JOBS: POPULATION BALANCE, AS PART OF THE FIVE-YEAR UPDATE. - 6:22 P.M.**

Mayor Toor moved, seconded by Poinsatte, to adopt the proposed changes to the Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan (BVCP) including the modified changes submitted by the County Commissioners regarding the Jobs: Population Balance, as part of the five-year update.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Poinsatte, to modify language to add “Given current policies” to beginning of second sentence and second paragraph in County Commissioner’s recommended changes. The motion carried: 5-0. Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel absent.

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Poinsatte to amend to combine the last two sentences of paragraph two, striking the words, “a project to determine whether or not the 1:1 ratio is appropriate. This will be accomplished through.” The motion failed: 2:3. Poinsatte and Toor opposed. Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel absent.

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Havlick to change the work “project” to “public process” in paragraph two. The motion carried: 5:0. Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to adopt the proposed changes to the Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan (BVCP) including the modified changes submitted by the County Commissioners regarding the Jobs: Population ratio as amended. The motion carried: 3:2. Havlick and Riggle opposed. Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel absent.

#### **B. CONSIDERATION OF GENERAL PROPOSED TEXT CHANGES TO THE BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (BVCP) AS PART OF THE FIVE-YEAR UPDATE. ## **10. SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE No. 7144 AMENDING THE DEVELOPMENT EXCISE TAX (DET) TO ESTABLISH CRITERIA FOR GRANTING A WAIVER OF THE DET ON CERTAIN PERMANENTLY AFFORDABLE HOUSING UNITS. - 7:50 P.M.**

## **11. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION APPROVING A PROPOSED NEXT-STEPS AND ACTIVITIES PROGRAM FOR SOUTH BOULDER CREEK FLOODPLAIN MANAGEMENT AND MITIGATION STUDY. - 8:12 P.M.**

The public hearing was opened:

1. John Bates, 862 Cypress Dr.—More study is not enough! Urged re-mapping of the west valley overflow into the regulatory flood zone (attachment).
2. Dorretta Hultquist, 1561 South Foothills Hwy E-5—Emergency Response Plan needs to be handled immediately. Accurate hydrology a must!
3. Robert Sharpe, 5995 Marshall Dr.—Applauded the citizen process. Urged serious consideration and action by Council.
4. Ruth Blackmore, 705 S. 41<sup>st</sup> St.—New Hydrology needed and we need to move on with this now (attachment).
5. Edward Smelko, 220 Seminole Dr.—CAG member. Opposed to channelization of creek between Highways 93 and 36. It would make things worse downstream.
6. Ellen Willis, 2895 Lafayette Dr.—Urged no further development in the floodplain.
7. Ben Binder, 720 S. 41<sup>st</sup>—Raised numerous issues regarding destructive actions.
8. Naomi Rachel, 954 Amery Choco [sic]—Opposed to floodplain development at Flatirons property. Urged support of the report by taking action to get accurate hydrology immediately.
9. David Albee, 1453 Marshall Rd.—Supported proceeding with a local Floodplain Study ASAP.

{5}------------------------------------------------

10. John Andrews, 1407 Kennedy Ave.—Urged caution in allowing the hospital to build in the floodplain.
11. John Weiner, 135 Manhattan Dr.—Encouraged public education regarding flood insurance available through Federal Government.

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Riggle, to adopt a proposed next-steps and activities program for South Boulder Creek Floodplain Management and Mitigation Study planning. The motion carried: 5:0 . Eldridge, Lopez, Mock and Morzel absent.
