## **4. MATTERS FROM THE CITY MANAGER:**

### **A. PRESENTATION OF THE 2001 LATINO COMMUNITY ASSESSMENT. - 6:30 p.m.**

Benita Duran and Pete Salas presented follow up to the Latino Community Assessment study. Mr. Salas pointed out highlights of the survey that addressed topics such as, education levels of community latinos show that 25% have a bachelors degree or higher and 40% hold a second job, showing they are a strong contributor to community economics. Suggestions from the presenters were to form latino action or focus groups to help encourage participation on City Boards and Commissions. Mentioned, also was the language barrier that needs to be addressed. Councilmember Havlick asked about expanding this type of group assessment to other groups of community members. The presenters felt this study would easily lead to others like it for community groups within the City.

## **6. PUBLIC HEARING AND CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION THAT THE ABSTRACT AND RESULT SHEETS BE APPROVED, AND SIGNING OF SAME IF APPROVED.**

1. Sheila Horton, 2940 Island Rd. Expressed concern for the integrity of this years mail in ballot election., siting what she believed to be numerous accounts of mis-handled or mis counted ballots. Her position is one of officially challenging the election.
2. Charlotte Houston, Boulder County Clerk and Recorder. Global Election Systems leased software to the County for the City/County election, as did Accuvote, a certified software for elections with a history of many previous elections. The trays of ballots in question were not missing or counted twice or not counted at all. The perception of Ms. Horton was perhaps due to the fact that neither she nor any other member of her organization were present during the entire election process. The results reflect that in deed the number of ballots voted and the number of ballots counted match.
3. Linda Flack. Chief Deputy County Clerk and Recorder. Explained the process if teams of election officials log the process and record any problems, which makes an accurate and easy to follow audit trail. Any register voter can all the County and see that their vote was indeed counted. This is not to say that how an individual voted is a matter of record but that all counted votes have an assigned number to be sure that all votes were accounted for.
4. Jeeb Campbell, 4395 Grinnell. Doesn’t like the mail in ballot process, prefers to get out and meet his neighbors at the polling place.
5. Lynn Segal, 538 Dewey. Relayed a story of a woman and her mother who had never before voted but because of the ease of mail ballot, did in fact vote this time. It was a great feeling and she supported the mail ballot process.

Board member Corson moved, seconded by Lopez, to approve the abstract and result sheets of the November 6, 2001 General Municipal Election for the City of Boulder. The motion carried: 6-0.

## **11. SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE No. 7169 AMENDING THE CONDITIONAL HEIGHT PROVISIONS FOR THE RB1-E AND RB1-X ZONING DISTRICTS SET FORTH IN SECTION 9-3.2-4, B.R.C., 1981, AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO. - 9:20 P.M.**

The public hearing was opened:

1. Michelle Goren, 844 19<sup>th</sup> St. Supporting the recommendation of the Ordinance to restrict height.
2. Susan Wilson, 2727 Pine St. In favor of the amendments of Ordinance 7169.
3. Christian Isquierdo, 1111 Maxwell #207. As a member of the Boulder Independent Business alliance Christian felt the changes to this Ordinance could have a critical impact on local business owners, believing that the changes would give larger chain business an advantage to build bigger.
4. Lauren Piscopo, 1035 Grant Pl. In favor of eliminating conditional height process. Wants to see the views preserved.

{7}------------------------------------------------

5. Richard Polk, 1425 Pearl St. Speaking on behalf of DMC. Mr. Polk read a letter previously written by DMC, expressing their recommendation to be not to change the conditional height provision. Also expressed support fo the West End Tavern.
6. Gwen Dooley, 730 Spruce. Supporting the Ordinance. Expressed surprise at the DMC finished product of the original Ordinance, in that she didn't remember discussion about a 45' section of the ordinance.
7. Nolan Rosall, 616 14<sup>th</sup> St. RRC. Association of Boulder. DBI is in support of the staff recommendations. Bonus items should be retained and 35' limit should read 38' for downtown.
8. John Spitzer, 2323 20<sup>th</sup> St. DDAP agrees that all corners should meet requirements. Is in support of the bonus system but cautions that part time residents taking advantage of the bonus could become the majority and this could have negative impacts on the residential downtown areas.
9. Lynn Segal, 538 Dewey. Small is beautiful.

Mayor Toor moved, seconded by Corson to amend Ordinance No. 7169 by amending the conditional height provisions for the RB1 zones to permit such additions only if the site is located within the RB1-X zone, that the site is surrounded by sites zoned RB1-X or P-E, and that the property is not in the historic district.. The motion carried: 6-2, Morzel and Riggle opposed, Eldridge recused.

INTRODUCTION, FIRST READING, AND CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO ORDER PUBLISHED BY TITLE ONLY AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2001 BUDGET TO ELIMINATE THE 2001 APPROPRIATION OF \$23,900,000 FOR THE LAKEWOOD RAW WATER PIPELINE PROJECT AND THE 2001 APPROPRIATION OF \$2,180,750 FOR THE BOND RESERVE AND REPLACE THEM WITH APPROPRIATIONS OF \$26,500,000 FOR THE LAKEWOOD RAW WATER PIPELINE PROJECT, \$104,735 FOR THE BOND COST OF ISSUANCE, AND \$2,225,265 FOR THE BOND RESERVE WITHIN THE WATER UTILITY FUND. THE SOURCE OF THESE APPROPRIATIONS IS THE \$28,830,000 IN PROCEEDS FROM THE CITY OF BOULDER WATER AND SEWER REVENUE BONDS, SERIES 2001. AND TO REDUCE THE**

{8}------------------------------------------------

#### **APPROPRIATION FROM THE WATER UTILITY FUND BALANCE BY \$1,755,416 TO \$29,359 WHICH IS THE 2001 DEBT SERVICE ON THE SERIES 2001 BONDS.**

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Eldridge to introduce and order published by title only Ordinance No. 7171 amending the 2001 budget to eliminate the 2001 appropriation of \$23,900,000 for the Lakewood Raw Water Pipeline project and the 2001 appropriation of \$2,180,750 for the bond reserve and replace them with appropriations of \$26,500,000 for the Lakewood Raw Water Pipeline project, \$104,735 for the bond cost of issuance, and \$2,225,265 for the bond reserve within the Water Utility Fund. The source of these appropriations is the \$28,830,000 in proceeds from the City of Boulder Water and Sewer Revenue Bonds, Series 2001. And to reduce the appropriation from the Water Utility Fund balance by \$1,755,416 to \$29,359 which is the 2001 debt service on the Series 2001 bonds. The motion carried 9-0.
