### **1. Call to Order and Roll Call**

Mayor Jones called the meeting to order at 6:03 p.m.

Council Member Yates moved to approve the Amended Agenda for the March 20, 2018 Meeting by adding Item 1B-Update on Long's Gardens Flood Mitigation Concept. Council Member Brockett seconded the motion. The motion passed 9:0 at 6:13 p.m.

- A. Declaration for the 40<sup>th</sup> Anniversary of the Bolder Boulder

- B. Update on Long's Gardens and Flood Mitigation Concept
