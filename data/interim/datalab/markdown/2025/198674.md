**use and lease a portion of the City of Boulder Fire Station Number 5 telecommunication facility on the property**

Council requested staff prepare a resolution of support for approval on consent at an upcoming meeting.

### **B. Council Discussion of City of Vinnytsia, Ukraine Sister City proposal and process**

Council elected with a “straw poll” to move this application forward and have the approval of the application schedule for an upcoming meeting on consent.

Council Members Speer and Wallach expressed their interest in being appointed as the liaison to Vinnytsia, Ukraine. Council Member Marquis expressed interest in being the “back-up” liaison. The Mayor requested CMs Speer and Wallach have an offline discussion to determine who will be appointed as the liaison and notify staff.
