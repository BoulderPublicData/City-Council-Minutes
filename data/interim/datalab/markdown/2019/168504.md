### **8. MATTERS FROM MAYOR AND MEMBERS OF COUNCIL**

- A. Review the tie-breaking process for board and commission appointments

City Attorney Tom Carr introduced this item at 11:50 p.m.

**Council chose to suspend the rules and if a tie occurs, then take a brief pause, enter into discussion and vote again. If the vote is still tied, then wait to address it at the end of the appointments. Vote again to see if the tie still exists.**

- B. Consideration of a motion to approve the 2019 Annual Boards and Commissions Appointments

Mayor Jones introduced this item at 11:53 p.m.

**City Clerk Lynnette Beck flipped the coin to see if the voting of candidates would proceed alphabetically or reverse alphabetically. The coin landed on “tails,” so the voting order began with reverse alphabetical order, then alphabetical, and so on. All seats were appointed by vote or acclamation, and after all seats were filled, council voted to ratify the appointments.**
