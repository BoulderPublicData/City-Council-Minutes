# **CITY OF BOULDER** **CITY COUNCIL MEETING** Municipal Building, 1777 Broadway Boulder, Colorado 80302 Tuesday, November 19, 2019 10 a.m.

## **MINUTES**

### 1. Call to Order and Roll Call

Mayor Jones called the meeting to order and roll was taken.

Council Members Brockett, Carlisle, Nagle, Morzel, Weaver, Yates and Young were present.

#### A. Swearing in of New Council Members

New Council Members Rachel Friend, Junie Joseph, Adam Swetlik and Mark Wallach and re-elected council members Aaron Brockett and Bob Yates were sworn in and approached the dais.

Departing Council Members Cindy Carlisle, Mayor Suzanne Jones, and Lisa Morzel left the dais.

Council Member Mary Young, who was the most senior council member not seeking re-election, chaired the rest of the meeting.

### 2. Matters from the Mayor and Members of Council

#### A. Consideration of a motion to continue the November 19, 2019, 10 a.m. meeting at 6 p.m.
