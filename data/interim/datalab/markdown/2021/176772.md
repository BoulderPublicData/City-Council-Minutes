- E. Consideration of a motion to amend the Annexation Agreement for 4395 Broadway within the Residential- Low 2 (RL-2) zoning district, related to the Shining Mountain Waldorf School redevelopment, to remove a two-story maximum above basement limitation on buildings for this property (LUR2020-00050)
- F. Introduction, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only Ordinance 8457 amending Section 3-1-1, “Definitions,” and adding a new Section 3-2-10, “Market Place Sales,” B.R.C. 1981, to assist in facilitating taxation on remote sales into the city and to authorize the city manager to enter into a Sales and Use Tax System Agreement, in substantially the same form as attached to the Agenda Memorandum, for state collection of sales tax from Marketplace Facilitators; and setting forth related details
- G. Introduction, first reading and consideration of a motion to order published by title only, Ordinance 8460 vacating and authorizing the city manager to execute a deed of vacation for a portion of an eight-foot alley bisecting the properties at 0 Pleasant and 1155 Pleasant St. (LUR2021-00006). The vacation will allow the sale of the two parcels for the purpose of creating a contiguous building site for the development of the Hill hotel
- H. Introduction, first reading and consideration of a motion to order published by title only, Ordinance 8461 vacating and authorizing the city manager to execute a deed of vacation for public right-of-way that bisects the northwest corner of the property at 3485 Stanford Ct., which was dedicated in 1957 for the extension of 35th Street (LUR2020-00054). This street is no longer planned or feasible given slopes and the existing development pattern

Second reading and consideration of a motion to adopt Ordinance 8456 to defer compliance of the Harvest of Hope Food Pantry (defined as a day shelter in the code) with the city’s development standards of Title 9, Land Use Code, for a period of three years to enable Harvest of Hope to begin needed operation of food distribution to those impacted by food insecurity at a new facility located at 4830 Pearl Street within the Industrial - General (IG) zoning district
