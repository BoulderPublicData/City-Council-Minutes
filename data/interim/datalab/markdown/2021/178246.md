## **8. Matters from the Mayor and Members of Council**

- A. Discussion on Council resolution(s) opposing/supporting the 2021 Ballot Measures  
Council Member Yates outlined why it is important for Council to have this discussion.

Council directed staff to prepare resolutions in support of the City’s five (5) Council referred ballot measures and bring forward for a vote on the October 19<sup>th</sup> “Consent” agenda .

- B. Discussion on Council meetings for the remainder of this Council’s term – In-Person or Virtual

Nuria Rivera-Vandermyde outlined the steps in progress to facilitate in-person or hybrid meetings. She also outlined her concerns with in-person meetings as it relates to staff concerns and COVID.

Council Member Yates proposed that this entire Council meet for the November 9<sup>th</sup> meeting and ask the new Council Members to meet in-person on November 16<sup>th</sup> and
