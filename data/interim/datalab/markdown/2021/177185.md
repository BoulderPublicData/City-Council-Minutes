### **7. Matters from the Mayor and Members of Council**

#### **A. In-person Council Meeting Discussion**

City Manager Nuria Rivera-Vandermyde outlined the issues staff faced with setting up the hybrid meeting environment. She confirmed that those issues have been resolved and chambers is ready when Council is to return to in-person. She noted the current state of COVID and the numbers rising on cases. This is a concern to not only the City but the entire County. Nuria also noted staff’s concerns about returning to the office environment with cases rising. And, that the City Manager’s Office is looking for input and guidance from Council on next steps as it pertains to being in-person.

Council commended staff on all the work that has been done and the update on the City’s approach to the latest COVID developments. After discussion, Council agreed to NOT have the August 17 meeting in-person and for the safety of all, to consider the decision to go back to in-person meetings on a meeting-by-meeting basis.
