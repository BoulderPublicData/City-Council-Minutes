## **4. Call-Up Check-In**

- A. **Call-Up Item: Site Review (case no. LUR2020-00052) to partially redevelop a portion of the property at 3485 Stanford Court** with sixty permanently affordable

{2}------------------------------------------------

apartments for seniors (age 55+) in one new building. The proposed units include 54 one-bedroom units and 6 two-bedroom units. The original sanctuary building is proposed to be landmarked and renovated for the existing childcare (Rainbow Childcare Center) on the lower level and new community space for residents on the upper level. The proposal includes a request for an increase in maximum allowable building height to allow a height of 45’-10” for the proposed building

Sloane Walbert, Senior Planner, provided a presentation and answered questions from council. Ian Shallow of Boulder Housing Partners also answered questions presented by Council.
