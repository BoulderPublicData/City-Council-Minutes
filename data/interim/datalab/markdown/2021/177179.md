## **3. Consent Agenda**

- A. **Introduction**, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8462** amending Chapter 3-20, “Rental License Excise Tax,” and Chapter 12-2, “Landlord – Tenant Relations,” B.R.C. 1981, making changes to implement the provisions related to eviction prevention and rental assistance services; and setting forth related details
- B. **Introduction**, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8458** amending Chapter 3-19, “Electronic Smoking Device Tax,” 4-32, “Tobacco Retailers,” 6-4, “Regulation of Smoking,” and 6-4.5, “Sale of Tobacco Products,” and Section 4-20-75, “Tobacco Retailer License Fee,” B.R.C. 1981, to clarify licensing of tobacco and electronic smoking device retailers and tax on electronic smoking devices by all businesses; and setting forth related details.

Council Members Brockett requested clarification on how the tax would affect items sold in Marijuana retailers. Staff Member Joel Wagner, Tax and Special Projects Manager provided clarification with a presentation. Council Member Wallach had questions related to device costs and where the tax will benefit the city. Tom Carr clarified who would be affected by the tax.

- C. **Second reading** and consideration of a motion to order published by title only, **Ordinance 8463** approving supplemental appropriations to the 2021 Budget.

Council Members Brockett, Friend and Swetlik had concerns about the additional funding related to the Police Department and voted NAY on item 3C.

## **4. Call-Up Check-In**

- A. **Application to Rename** the **“Settler’s Park” trailhead** and park area in west Boulder to **“The Peoples’ Crossing”**

Staff members Dan Burke, Director of Open Space and Mountain Parks and Phillip Yates, Communications Specialist, provided Council with a presentation outlining the history of this item and acknowledgment of the collaboration between staff and tribal representatives. Council expressed it’s appreciation for the all the work that has been done to get this accomplished with full Council support.

**NO ACTION**

- B. **Call up: Site Review Amendment** for a new 11,534 square foot dining and commons building located on the campus of the **Boulder Country Day School at 4820 Nautilus Court North**. The Applicant is seeking a 62% parking reduction. Case No. LUR2019-00071.

**NO ACTION**

- C. **Call-Up Consideration Item: Nonconforming Use Review** to permit renovation and reconfiguration of an existing three-story, 18,469 square foot, 24-unit apartment building at **1024 Marine Street** into two, interconnected buildings and 18 attached dwelling units with a new ground floor common area. The existing building and site are nonconforming to density, open space and parking. Reviewed under case #LUR2020-00020.

**NO ACTION**
