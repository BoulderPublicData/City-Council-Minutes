## **3. Consent Agenda**

- A. Consideration of a motion to approve **May 4, 2021 Regular Meeting Minutes**
- B. Consideration of a motion to accept the **Study Session Summary from the April 27, 2021 Study Session regarding the Annual Financial Update**
- C. Consideration of a motion to accept the **April 27, 2021 Study Session Summary regarding the YOAB Action Team Accomplishments**
- D. Consideration of a motion to accept the **April 27, 2021 Study Session Summary regarding Safe Space Management of Public Areas and Sanctioned Camping**
- E. Consideration of a motion to approve the **Citywide Retail Strategy considering effects of the COVID-19 pandemic and related strategy implementation to date**
- F. **Introduction**, first reading and consideration of a motion to order published by title only, **Ordinance 8463** approving annual carryover and supplemental appropriations to the 2021 Budget
- G. **Introduction**, first reading and consideration of a motion to order published by title only, **Ordinance 8436** amending Section 6-14-13, “Prohibited Acts,” and 6-16-13, “Prohibited Acts,” B.R.C. 1981, to allow the sale of consumable hemp-infused products at marijuana retail businesses; and setting forth related details
- H. **Introduction**, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8455** amending Section 5-7-2, “Possession and Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Prohibited,” B.R.C. 1981, in order to allow enforcement of alcohol restrictions on OSMP-managed land; and setting forth related details
- I. **Introduction**, first reading and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8466** designating a portion of the property at 96 Arapahoe Ave. as an individual landmark per Section 9-11-5 of the Boulder Revised Code, 1981.
- J. **Introduction**, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8464** which would repeal Boulder Revised Code 11-4-3, Aircraft Permitted At Airport

{2}------------------------------------------------

- K. **Second reading** and consideration of a motion to adopt **Ordinance 8457** amending Section 3-1-1, “Definitions,” and adding a new Section 3-2-10, “Market Place Sales,” B.R.C. 1981, to assist in facilitating taxation on remote sales into the city and to authorize the city manager to enter into a Sales and Use Tax System Agreement, in substantially the same form as attached to the Agenda Memorandum, for state collection of sales tax from Marketplace Facilitators; and setting forth related details
- L. **Second reading** and consideration of a motion to adopt **Ordinance 8460** vacating and authorizing the city manager to execute deeds of vacation for a portion of an eight-foot alley bisecting the properties at 0 Pleasant and 1155 Pleasant St. (LUR2021-00006). The vacation will allow the sale of the two parcels for the purpose of creating a contiguous building site for the development of the Hill hotel
- M. **Second reading** and consideration of a motion to adopt **Ordinance 8461** vacating and authorizing the city manager to execute a deed of vacation for public right-of-way that bisects the northwest corner of the property at 3485 Stanford Ct., which was dedicated in 1957 for the extension of 35th Street (LUR2020-00054). This street is no longer planned or feasible given slopes and the existing development pattern.
- N. **Continuation of City Council’s consideration of a Nonconforming Use Review for the historic restoration and conversion of the former Marpa House** group living quarters at 891 12th Street into 16 three-bedroom dwelling units with a maximum occupancy of 48 persons. The project site is zoned Residential - Low 1 (RL-1). Case no. LUR2020-00022.
- O. **City Attorney Recruitment Subcommittee Update and Motion to appoint Sandra Llanes as interim city attorney and to authorize the city manager to enter into a contract**
