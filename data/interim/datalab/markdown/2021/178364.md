# **CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING**

**Via Video Conferencing**

**Tuesday, October 12, 2021**

## **MINUTES**

## **1. Call to Order and Roll Call:**

Mayor Weaver called the meeting to order at **6:01** p.m.

Council Members present virtually: Brockett, Friend, Joseph, Nagle, Swetlick, Wallach, Weaver, Yates and Young

- A. Declaration **honoring Mary Duvall** upon her retirement

## **2. Consent Agenda**

- A. Consideration of a motion to authorize the City Manager to **execute an Intergovernmental Agreement**, in substantially the same form as attached to the Agenda Memorandum, **with Boulder Housing Partners on City Property at Alpine-Balsam** to guide redevelopment of the property into a mixed-use, pedestrian-oriented neighborhood which complies with the city’s vision, area plan, and regulations ensuring access to transit, a high percentage of affordable housing, and public spaces that benefit the entire community.
- B. Consideration of a motion to **adopt Resolutions 1296, 1297, 1298 and 1299** indicating the City Council’s support of Ballot Measures 2I, 2J, 2K, 2L and 2M appearing on the November 2, 2021, ballot.
- C. **Introduction**, first reading, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8509 amending Sections 2-2-15, “Neighborhood Permit Parking Zones,”** and 4-20-49, “Neighborhood Parking Permit Fee,” Chapters 4-23, “Neighborhood Parking Zone Permits,” and 7-6, “Parking Infractions,” B.R.C. 1981, in order to implement access management and parking strategies; and setting forth related details.
