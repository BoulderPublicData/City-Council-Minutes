## **10. PUBLIC HEARING AND CONSIDERATION OF ADOPTION OF REVISED BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (BVCP) POLICIES FOR THE FIVE-YEAR UPDATE. - 7:43 p.m.**

Public Hearing:

- 1. Judith Renfroe - Concerned about policy change
- 2. Rob Smoke, 5995 Marshall Dr. - re: Concerned about policy changes and public input process.

{6}------------------------------------------------

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Morzel to approve Sections 1.01 to 3.20 of revised Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan (BVCP) policies for the five-year update as amended.

### **1. GENERAL POLICIES**

#### **p. A-2 Principles of Sustainability**

Add united nations definition: sustainable development meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

#### **p. A-4 1.07 Regional Cooperation**

Add “jobs-housing balance” in the first sentence.

#### **p. A-5 1.14 Growth Requirements**

In the last sentence, replace “from” with “at the time of” annexation.

##### **p. A-8 1.20(d) Annexation**

Undelete “Area II”.

### **2. COMMUNITY DESIGN**

#### **p. A-14 Preservation of Community Character**

Delete “which may include regulating demolitions and out of scale new homes and additions.”

#### **p. A-15 Accessory Units**

Delete “and without increasing cumulative negative impacts on neighborhoods”

#### **p. A-17 2.21 Urban Open Lands**

Replace “ seek to provide” with “promote and maintain”

##### **p. A-20 2.29 Tree Conservation**

bring back revised language to include reference to wildlife habitat.

### **3. FACILITIES AND SERVICES**

#### **p. A-24 3.05 Growth to Pay Fair Share of New Facility Costs**

Change 2<sup>nd</sup> sentence to “When permitting additional development, the city shall consider whether public facilities are....”

#### **p . A-25 3.12 Parks and Recreation**

Add at the end of the 1<sup>st</sup> sentence: “...for a broad spectrum of Boulder citizens.”

#### **p. A-26 Aesthetic Elements in Public Projects**

{7}------------------------------------------------

Change “aesthetic” to artistic in title and text and delete “all”

##### **Canyon Boulevard Cultural Corridor**

Delete “all”

p. A-27      **3.18 Planning for School Sites and Facilities**

Add new sentence: “The city and county shall also consider transportation impacts when planning for school sites and facilities.”

The motion carried 7-1. Lopez opposed. Havlick absent.

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Mock to table to 11/21/00, Sections 4.01 to 8.1 of revised Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan (BVCP) policies for the five-year update. The motion carried 8-0. Havlick absent.
