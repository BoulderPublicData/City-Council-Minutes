# **CITY OF BOULDER CITY COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS September 19, 2000**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL - 6:00 p.m.**

Mayor Toor called the regular September 19, 2000 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Mock, Lopez, Morzel, Poinsatte, and Riggle. Councilmembers Eldridge and Havlick were absent.

### Staff /Council Response:

Councilmember Morzel requested a report regarding further treatments for the intersections of Valmont and Airport and Valmont and Kings Ridge, as well as a report regarding a time signal at the intersection of Iris and 28th.

Councilmember Mock clarified that the Saturday Meter changes came from requests from business owners who had customer complaints about lack of parking; also clarified that the development excise tax was approved by the voters at it's current level after having failed at the polls the previous year at a higher level.

Councilmembers Lopez and Morzel provided an update regarding South Boulder Creek Flood Plain Study. A Study Session will be held on this issue either 10/10/00 or 10/24/00.

Mayor Toor asked staff to look in to whether adequate handicap parking spaces exist downtown, what the criteria is, and also asked for historical research regarding charging for handicap parking.

### **E. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION APPROVING THE JOINT PURCHASE WITH BOULDER COUNTY OF 148.73 ACRES OWNED BY THE CITO COMPANY LOCATED ON GUNBARREL HILL, JUST SOUTH OF MINERAL ROAD AND EAST OF 79<sup>TH</sup> STREET FOR OPEN SPACE PURPOSES.**

## **4. MATTERS FROM THE CITY MANAGER - 6:55 p.m.**

### **A. REPORT ON THE BOULDER VALLEY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN UPDATE PROCESS.**

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Riggle, to request that Planning Board evaluate the elimination of the Planning Reserve by changing it to a Rural Preserve in addition to the current proposal to change to 159 acres of Planning Reserve to Rural Preserve adopted by Council on September 5, 2000.

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Mock , to adopt Option No. 3, to defer Land Use Map changes for Area Plans.

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Poinsatte, to direct staff to look at the potential for reducing Commercial Land Use Designations in Area 1 (including options for reducing job growth).

## **5. MATTERS FROM THE CITY ATTORNEY - None - 7:55**

## **6. MATTERS FROM MAYOR AND MEMBERS OF COUNCIL:**

### **A. CONSIDERATION OF A RESOLUTION ENCOURAGING THE CITIZENS OF THE CITY OF BOULDER TO OPPOSE AMENDMENT NO. 21 ALSO KNOWN A“TAXCUT 2000,” WHEN VOTING IN THE NOVEMBER 7, 2000 ELECTION. - 7:55 p.m.**

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Morzel, to adopt Resolution No. 862. encouraging the citizens of the City of Boulder to oppose Amendment No. 21 also known as “Taxcut 2000,” when voting in the November 7, 2000 election.

{3}------------------------------------------------

### **B. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO SUPPORT BOULDER COUNTY BALLOT QUESTION 1B AUTHORIZING THE EXTENSION OF THE COUNTY'S EXISTING .10% RECYCLING AND COMPOSTING SALES AND USE TAX FOR EIGHT YEARS AND FOR THE ISSUANCE OF UP TO \$80,800,000 IN BONDS, FOR THE PURCHASE AND MAINTENANCE OF OPEN SPACE.- 7:58 p.m.**

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Morzel, to support Boulder County Ballot Question 1B authorizing the extension of the County's existing .10% recycling and composting sales and use tax for eight years and for the issuance of up to \$80,800,000 in bonds, for the purchase and maintenance of open space. The motion carried: 7-0, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

- B. Leo Hollby, 5052 North Foothills - re: Planning Reserve motion, as property owner does not want to be left out of Planning Reserve.
- C. Chuck Palmer, 2270 Bluebell Ave. - re: supports opposition to growth and supports County Open Space proposal.
- D. Garland Campbell, 4395 Grinnell - re: supports mixed use in Table Mesa/ Base Mar areas.

## **8. FINAL DECISIONS - 8:06 p.m.**

Vote was taken on the motion to approve Consent Agenda items 3-A through 3-E. The motion carried: 7-0, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to request that Planning Board evaluate the elimination of the Planning Reserve by changing it to a Rural Preserve in addition to the current proposal to change to 159 acres of Planning Reserve to Rural Preserve adopted by Council on September 5, 2000. The motion carried: 5-2, Corson and Lopez opposed, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to adopt Option No. 3, to defer Land Use Map changes for Area Plans. The motion carried: 4-3, Corson, Lopez and Poinsatte opposed, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to direct staff to look at the potential for reducing Commercial Land Use Designations in Area 1 (including options for reducing job growth). The motion carried: 6 - 1, Lopez opposed, Eldridge and Havlick.

{4}------------------------------------------------

Vote was taken on the motion to adopt Resolution No. 862, encouraging the citizens of the City of Boulder to oppose Amendment No. 21 also known as "Taxcut 2000," when voting in the November 7, 2000 election. The motion carried: 7-0, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to support Boulder County Ballot Question 1B authorizing the extension of the County's existing .10% recycling and composting sales and use tax for eight years and for the issuance of up to \$80,800,000 in bonds, for the purchase and maintenance of open space. The motion carried: 7-0, Eldridge and Havlick absent.

## **9. SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 7078, ADOPTING CHAPTER 6-12, B.R.C. 1981, REGULATING TRASH HAULERS AND RECYCLABLE MATERIALS, AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO.**

### Public Hearing:

1. Philip (Mick) Mahoney, 5565 Ute Hwy - re: opposed as ordinance fails miserably- bad economies and bad government by price control.
2. Daniel Ziskin, 2202 Goss Circle - re: Jews of the Earth supports ordinance tremendously. Waste reduction necessary for sustainability.
3. Edwin Crow, 605 20<sup>th</sup> St2 - re: block leader for Eco-Cycle in support of proposed ordinance.

5. Karl Williamson, 5100 Euclid - re: also supports ordinance. We need to be innovative in finding solutions to ecological problems.
6. Eric Lombardi, 1348 Periwinkle Dr. - re: Executive Director of Eco-Cycle- this is the "right" thing to do, by directing the market, setting the rules.
