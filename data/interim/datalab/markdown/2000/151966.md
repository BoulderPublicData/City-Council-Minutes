# **CITY OF BOULDER CITY COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS June 20, 2000**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Toor called the regular June 20, 2000 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Havlick, Mock, Morzel, Poinsatte, and Riggle. Councilmember Lopez was absent.

### **A. SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF DOROTHY RUPERT.**

Mayor Toor proclaimed June 20, 2000 as Dorothy Rupert Day in Boulder in honor of her numerous contributions to the community. Due to term limits, Ms. Rupert is retiring from the Senate.

## **2. CITIZEN PARTICIPATION and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE**

## **4. MATTERS FROM THE CITY MANAGER: - 7:05 p.m.**

### **A. DISCUSSION REGARDING THE STATUS OF CURRIGAN HALL.**

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Morzel, to respond to Denver letting them know that due to risks being higher than anticipated, City of Boulder cannot pursue obtaining Currigan Hall at this time but supports preservation of the building and urges their consideration of other

{4}------------------------------------------------

proposals. Further, that if no other parties are identified, the City of Boulder may still be interested in the future.

## 5. **MATTERS FROM THE CITY ATTORNEY:** - 7:40 p.m.

The City Attorney reported that in light of the recent Supreme Court case of Crosby v. National Foreign Trade Council, Council will have to consider repealing the Burma selective purchasing order. The City Attorney's office is working with the proponents of the original ordinance and will report back in July.

## 6. **MATTERS FROM MAYOR AND MEMBERS OF COUNCIL:** - 7:45 p.m.

### **A. POTENTIAL CALL-UP:**

## 7. **PUBLIC COMMENT:** - None - 7:55 p.m.

## 8. **FINAL DECISIONS:** - 7:57 p.m.

Vote was taken on the motion to approve Consent Agenda items 3-A through 3-G as amended to include Emergency Ordinance No. 7066 for Item 3-G (1). The motion carried: 8-0, Lopez absent.

Vote was taken on the motion to respond to Denver letting them know that due to risks being higher than anticipated, City of Boulder cannot pursue obtaining Currigan Hall at this time but supports preservation of the building and urges their consideration of other proposals. Further, that if no other parties are identified, the City of Boulder may still be interested in the future. The motion carried: 8-0, Lopez absent.

## **ORDER OF BUSINESS**

### **PUBLIC HEARINGS**

9. **SECOND READING AND CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 7054 TO APPROVE AN INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT WITH BOULDER COUNTY, CITY OF LOUISVILLE, TOWN OF SUPERIOR AND THE CITY OF BOULDER ENTITLED, "US 36 CORRIDOR COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN".** - 8:00 p.m.

{5}------------------------------------------------

There being no comments from the general public, the hearing was closed.

Councilmember Morzel moved, seconded by Corson, to adopt Ordinance No. 7054 to approve an Intergovernmental Agreement with Boulder County, City of Louisville, Town of Superior and the City of Boulder entitled, “US 36 Corridor Comprehensive Development Plan”. The motion carried:8-0, Lopez absent.

## **10. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO APPROVE THE COMMUNITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT PROCESS (CEAP) FOR THE 28<sup>TH</sup> STREET IMPROVEMENTS FROM BASELINE ROAD TO ARAPAHOE AVENUE (SOUTH SEGMENT). - 8:05 p.m.**

The public hearing was opened:

1. Dave Allured, 4231 Eaton Court - re: representing requests from the Boulder Bicycle Commuters Association.
2. Adam Krom, 3626 Broadway - Representing 28<sup>th</sup> Street Design Committee. Spoke in support of the South Segment CEAP. Raised issues with Colorado Avenue changes.
3. Ricky Weiser, 4020 N. 75<sup>th</sup> - re: 1) Opposed to destruction of trees South of Colorado, 2) Concerned about access to shopping center South of Safeway, and 3) Bike access South of Colorado very dangerous.
4. Premena, P.O Box 1038 - re: advocated on-street bike lanes. Conflict of right turning traffic on College with bike path.
5. Krista Holland, 3116 47<sup>th</sup> Street - re: also advocated allowing bikes on 28<sup>th</sup> with shoulders.

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Corson, to approve the 28<sup>th</sup> Street CEAP (South Segment) “Hello Boulder” with consideration to be given to suggestions raised by Council. The motion carried: 8-0, Lopez absent.

### **FIRST READINGS:**
