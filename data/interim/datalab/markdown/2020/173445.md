# **CITY OF BOULDER** **CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING** **Via Video Conferencing** Tuesday, May 12, 2020

## **MINUTES**

### 1. Call to Order and Roll Call

Mayor Weaver called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Council Members Brockett, Friend, Joseph, Nagle, Swetlick, Wallach, Yates and Young were present.

**COUNCIL MEMBER YOUNG MADE A MOTION TO APPROVE THE AMENDED AGENDA TO ADD ITEMS 1C, 4A AND 4B. COUNCIL MEMBER WALLACH SECONDED THE MOTION. THE MOTION PASSED 9:0 AT 6:03 P.M.**

#### A. COVID-19 Briefing: Pubic Health/Boulder Community Hospital

Jeff Zayach from Boulder County Health provided updated information regarding COVID-19 cases in Boulder County (case load, long term care facility increase, 5 day averages and by race).

Boulder County Medical Director Dr. Chris Urbina joined the meeting to discuss the Strategic Testing Roadmap and its focus on symptomatic community members and contacts of those individuals.

Chief Medical Officer Boulder Community Health Dr. Ben Keidan joined the meeting to give updates on daily census at Boulder Community Health, testing and COVID experience and the significant capacity that currently exists at the hospital.

#### B. COVID-19 Response Discussion

Assistant City Manager and Community Vitality Director Yvette Bowden provided a high level overview of the work being done in response to the needs of the restaurant industry.

Police Chief Maris Herold provided an update on public health order violations and enforcement/education of face coverings and gatherings.

Open Space and Mountain Parks Director Dan Burke provided an update on the department's response to the face covering order issued by Boulder County.
