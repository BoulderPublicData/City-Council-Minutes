City Manager Nuria Rivera-Vandermyde acknowledge the impact of the fire on our community and a brief overview of the events surrounding the fire that included, notification, evacuation and sheltering, numbers, no loss of life or structures, containment, other agency support, and the mitigation work done by the City’s Open Space and Mountain Parks.
