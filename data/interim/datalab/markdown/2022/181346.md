3. Continued second reading and consideration of a **motion to continue Ordinance 8540** submitting to the registered electors of the City of Boulder at the Special Municipal Coordinated Election to be held on Tuesday, November 8, 2022, the question of **whether to amend Sections 5 and 9 of the Boulder Home Rule Charter to prohibit running for more than one office at an election, allow a council member whose term does not expire at the election to run for mayor in the election, fill a vacancy for the expired term, and change the swearing-in date of newly elected officials**; specifying the form of the ballot and other election procedures; and setting forth related details
