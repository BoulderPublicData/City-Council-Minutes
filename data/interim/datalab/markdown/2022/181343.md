City Clerk, Elesha Johnson, flipped a coin to see who would speak first. Heads was Council Member Speer and Council Member Wallach was tails. The coin landed on heads, and Council member Speer spoke first.

Council members Folkerts, Yates, Joseph, Mayor Brockett, Winer, Benjamin and Friend provided statements.

The City Clerk flipped a coin to determine which candidate will be voted on first. It was heads and Council Member Speer’s vote went first.

Results: 3 Council Members (Folkerts, Speer and Joseph), raised their hand in favor for Council Member Speer and 6 Council Members (Benjamin, Brockett, Friend, Wallach, Winer, and Yates), raised their hands in favor for Council member Wallach.

Council Members Speer and Wallach offered gratitude to all the support they received.

Council member Wallach was elected as Mayor Pro-Tem for a 1-year term.
