## **2. Consent Agenda**

- A. ~~Consideration of a motion to accept the April 26, 2022 Study Session Summary regarding Wildfire Protection and Resiliency~~ - **REMOVED**
- B. Consideration of a motion to **accept** the **April 26, 2022 Study Session Summary** regarding the **Draft 2022 Boulder Parks and Recreation Master Plan**
- C. Consideration of the **following proposed ordinances related to firearm restrictions** in the City of Boulder:

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8494** amending Chapter 5, “General Offenses,” B.R.C. 1981, repealing Ordinances 8245 and 8259, banning the sale and possession of assault weapons, large-capacity magazines and rapid-fire trigger activators, raising the age to purchase firearms, amending Section 5-2-26, “Matters of Local and Mixed Concern,” B.R.C. 1981, and adding a new Section 5-8-42, “Severability,” B.R.C. 1981; and setting forth related details;

#### **AND/OR**

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8525** repealing and reenacting Section 5-8-15, “Deadly Weapons in City Buildings Prohibited,” B.R.C. 1981, to prohibit the carrying of firearms in certain public places; and setting forth related details;

#### **AND/OR**

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8526** amending Chapter 5-8, “Weapons,” B.R.C. 1981, by adding new definitions, and a new Section 5-8-39, “Prohibitions,” B.R.C. 1981, to regulate the possession of unfinished frames and receivers, and unserialized firearms; and setting forth related details;

#### **AND/OR**

{2}------------------------------------------------

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8527** repealing and reenacting Section 5-8-21, “Open Carriage of Firearms in Carrying Cases Required,” B.R.C. 1981, to prohibit the open carrying of firearms in public places; and setting forth related details;

#### **AND/OR**

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8528** amending Chapter 5-8, “Weapons,” B.R.C. 1981, by adding new definitions, and a new Section 5-8-40, “Disclosure,” B.R.C. 1981, requiring all firearm dealers to post signs at all locations where firearms transfers take place; and setting forth related details;

#### **AND/OR**

Introduction, **first reading**, and consideration of a motion to order published by title only **Ordinance 8529** amending Chapter 5-8, “Weapons,” B.R.C. 1981, by adding a new definition, and a new Section 5-8-41, “Waiting Period Prior to Firearm Purchase,” B.R.C. 1981, requiring a waiting period prior to the sale of firearms; and setting forth related details

Luis Torro, Senior Attorney, provided a presentation that detailed each Gun Violence Prevention Ordinance and answered questions from Council. He was supported by Allison Anderman of Giffords Law Center and Dustin Williamson, Counsel for “Every Town for Gun Safety”.

- D. Consideration of a motion to **call a Special Meeting** of the Boulder City Council on **Tuesday, May 24th, 2022**
