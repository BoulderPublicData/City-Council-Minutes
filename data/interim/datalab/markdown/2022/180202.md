Teresa Taylor-Tate, City Attorney and Kathy Haddock, Senior Counsel, spoke on this item and answered questions from Council.

Council supported separating all things related to changing to even year election to one ballot question and clean up items to a separate ballot question.

Council decided to have a later conversation regarding the mayoral election ballot item.
