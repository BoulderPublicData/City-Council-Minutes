### **6. Matters from the City Manager**

### **A. COVID Update/Check-in** on return to in-person meetings

Pam Davis, Assistant City Manager/COVID-19 Response and Recovery Manager presented Council with the current CDC guidelines related to community risk levels, staff recommendations on timeline for returning to in-person Council meetings. She also noted that the city will continue to monitor Boulder County’s levels and “red” or “high” levels would warrant returning to virtual meetings. She was joined by James Brown, Risk Manager.

- x Regular and Special Meetings to convert to hybrid beginning in April.
- x Study Sessions, CAC and Committee meetings to remain virtual.
- x Boards and Commissions use their discretion of going hybrid or virtual one month after Council begins their hybrid format.

She also answered questions from Council.

Council elected to agree to staff’s recommendations but expressed concerns on returning to hybrid when numbers reach the “red” level. Council requested to consider that option at the “HOORZ” (mid-range) level being the threshold in stead.
