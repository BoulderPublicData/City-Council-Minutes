# **CITY COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS** **CITY OF BOULDER** **October 21, 2014**

## **1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL – 5:00 PM**

Mayor Appelbaum called the October 21, 2014 City Council meeting to order at 6:06 PM in Council Chambers.

Those present were: Mayor Appelbaum, Council Members Cowles, Jones, Karakelian, Morzel, Plass, Shoemaker, Weaver, and Young.

## **4. Report from Fire and Police on Mobile Home Fire**

Police Chief Greg Festa and Interim Fire Chief Michael Calderazzo gave an update to Council regarding the response to a recent mobile home fire in Boulder Meadows.

Council Member Cowles noted that anyone interested in donating to the firemen to assist them in their time of loss could do so at [www.getfunding.com/bxoyo](http://www.getfunding.com/bxoyo)

## **2. OPEN COMMENT and COUNCIL/STAFF RESPONSE – 6:28 PM**

1. Greg Wilkerson – Asked that Council commission a survey of Boulder citizens regarding the target population for the city at large. He proposed a target population of 120,000 by the year 2100.
2. John Herofort – Thanked the Council for their work on the electric assist bicycle pilot and in favor of removing the sunset date.
3. Carl Norby – Spoke about the environmental problems caused by leaking wastewater pipes. He was primarily concerned about the rising water table. He presented Council with a letter signed by citizens living in Brasier Meadows supporting the views he presented.
4. Patricia Multhaul – Thanked Council for increasing the utility budget in 2015 to continue flood recovery efforts. She echoed Mr. Norby's concerns regarding ground and wastewater.
