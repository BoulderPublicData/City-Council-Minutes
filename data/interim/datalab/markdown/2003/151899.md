#### **B. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION REGARDING THE INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF FIREFIGHTERS (IAFF) ARBITRATION REPORT. - 7:15P.M.**

City Manager Bruno introduced the item stating that his recommendation to approve the city's last best offer was difficult but necessary given the current budget crisis. He further stressed the need to protect the municipal corporation from further cuts noting that where police, fire and human services were being limited to 5-8% cuts, other departments throughout the organization are taking up to 25% cuts. Mr. Bruno also acknowledged the appreciation by the Council, himself and the community for the outstanding services provided by these important departments.

The public hearing was opened.

- 1 John Nunez - Vice President of Local Union 900 - acknowledged the commitment of his co-workers and then addressed concerns regarding the process. The Union did not feel the impasse resolution process could be honored if Council did not support the arbitrator's finding. Urged Council to honor the process.
- 2 Tom Buescher, 1563 Gaylord Street, Denver, 80206; Attorney for Local 900 - addressed the arbitrator's decision outlining reasons why that recommendation should be honored. Spoke of the need to treat all employee groups equitably.

Clarification from John Nunez re: 100% Health Coverage - this does not take into consideration the fact that co-pays have increased and level of coverage has decreased.

HROE Director Joann Roberts Stacy reviewed and presented comparisons between Management benefits and the 3 bargaining units.

There being no further speakers the public hearing was closed.

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Riggie, to implement the last best offer by the City to IAFF Local 900. The motion carried 6 0; Eldridge and Toor absent.

#### **C. REPORT ON DISCUSSIONS CONCERNING THE FLATIRONS PROPERTY/CU SOUTH. - 8:27P.M.**

City Manager Bruno indicated discussions with the University have focused on developing a draft covenant and MOU. The draft included in the packet does not reflect any agreement on proposals discussed by the City and CU. He further stated that he would not present a proposal to Council that does not fairly balance all of the issues.

Councilmember Havlick raised concern relating to the potential impacts of any discussion regarding use of the CU South property until after both the Hydrology study and the Floodplain Mitigation study reports are available to Council

Issue identity seems to be the benefit of discussions to date and there may be value in continuing discussions regarding issues not reliant on the outcome of the hydrology study.

City Manager Bruno offered to work with staff to bring back a proposal that would encompass the discussion and feedback provided by Council It was also suggested that the city invite the Regents to participate in an initial dialogue (Breakfast) with hopes of re-establishing regular interactions

Councilmember Havlick moved, seconded by Morzel to invite the CU Regents to participate in an initial dialogue with hopes of re-establishing regular interactions.
