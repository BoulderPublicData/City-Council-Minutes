Those present were: Mayor Toor and Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Havlick, Mock, Morzel, Poinsatte, Riggle and Ruzzin.

### **STAFF RESPONSE: - 6:50p.m.**

City Manager Bruno responded to several items raised:

1. He noted that several of the items were scheduled to be heard during the remainder of this meeting, including: 1) IPM recommendations, CU South update, Chautauqua Liquor License request, 429 Spruce Street, and Ordinance No. 7270 regarding Water Rates.
2. The bio-diesel information will be forwarded to the fleet staff and Mr. Bruno will follow-up on current fleet policies.
3. It was pointed out that Council recently authorized the use of Education Excise Tax funds to address field improvements at both Boulder High and Fairview High which will include the use of non-potable water. Staff will provide a W.I.P. item for Council which clarifies other areas within the city that use non-potable water.

City Attorney deRaismes reported that the issue regarding the 5.5% interest that landlords are required to pay on security deposits was voted on by the citizens and could only be changed by another vote of the citizens. He did indicate that there was some leeway within the text of the peoples ordinance but that it had to be at least 50% of the prime interest rate.

Mr. deRaismes also addressed Council regarding process options regarding the call-up of the demolition permit request for 429 Spruce Street.

### **C. DROUGHT STAGE - 9:10P.M.**

Carol Ellinghouse and Ned Williams provided an update regarding current snow pack levels and projections relative to this years water supply. City Manager Bruno announced that the drought stage at this time, due to the drought recovery need, is Drought Stage I.

### **D. LIBRARY PARKING UPDATE - 9:50P.M.**

City Manager Bruno announced that the pay for parking at the library site has been placed on hold to allow the manager and staff to address questions that have been raised by both the Council and citizens and to bring this forward in a comprehensive manner for Council consideration this summer. Council asked for an update prior to June 3<sup>rd</sup> regarding issues and progress on this matter.

## **8. FINAL DECISIONS: - 10:30p.m.**

Vote was taken on the motion to approve consent items 3A through 3K with items 3C, 3H and 3L removed. The motion carried 9:0.

The motion on the floor was to direct the City Manager to identify a public process and implement a public forum where the public can express its best ideas for where and how much C.U. should expand and then following the pubic forum reconvene negotiations between the City and C.U.

{6}------------------------------------------------

Councilmember Mock offered a substitute motion, accepted by Morzel and Havlick, to change the motion as follows:

A motion to direct the City Manager to conduct a public forum to solicit public input as to possible alternate development sites for C.U., to happen concurrently with discussions between the City and C.U. The motion carried 8:1; Eldridge opposed.

Vote was taken on the motion to approve the recommendations from the Integrated Pest Management Task Force. The motion carried 9:0.

Councilmember Mock moved, seconded by Ruzzin to suspend the rules and continue the meeting. The motion carried 6:4; Corson, Eldridge, Havlick and Riggle opposed.
