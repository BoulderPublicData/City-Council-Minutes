# **CITY OF BOULDER COUNCIL PROCEEDINGS November 18, 2003 10:00 a.m.**

## **1      CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL**

Mayor Toor called the special November 18, 2003 meeting of the Boulder City Council to order at 10:00 a.m. in the Council Chambers

Those present were: Councilmembers Corson, Eldridge, Morzel, Poinsatte, Riggle, Ruzzin and Toor. Councilmember Havlick was absent

### **A.      PRESENTATION OF CERTIFICATES OF APPRECIATION BY DOUG YOUNG OF CONGRESSMAN UDALL'S OFFICE. - 10:00A.M.**

Doug Young of Congressman Udall's office presented Certificates of Appreciation for service to outgoing councilmembers

### **B.      PARTING COMMENTS FROM COUNCIL. - 10:12A.M.**

Parting comments were given by outgoing Councilmembers Corson, Morzel and Poinsatte

## **2      ORDER OF BUSINESS/PUBLIC HEARING**

### **A.      CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO CONTINUE THE NOVEMBER 18, 2003 10:00A.M. SPECIAL MEETING AND 6:00P.M. REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOULDER CITY COUNCIL TO 6:00P.M. ON NOVEMBER 25, 2003. - 10:30A.M.**

Councilmember Corson moved, seconded by Morzel to continue the November 18, 2003 Special meeting and 6:00p.m. regular meeting of the Boulder City Council to 6.00p.m. on November 25, 2003. The motion carried 7-0, Havlick absent.

### **B.      CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION AMENDING RESOLUTION No. 933 DESIGNATING RUTH WRIGHT AS ADDITIONAL MEMBER ON THE GENERAL CANVASSING AND ELECTION BOARD FOR THE NOVEMBER 4, 2003 GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION. - 10:30A.M.**

Councilmember Riggle moved, seconded by Morzel to amend Resolution No. 933 designating Ruth Wright as additional member on the General Cavassing and Election Board for the November 4, 2003 general municipal election. The motion carried 7-0, Havlick absent.

{1}------------------------------------------------

## **ADJOURNMENT**

There being no further business to come before Council at this time, BY MOTION REGULARLY ADOPTED, THE MEETING WAS ADJOURNED AT 10:35 A M

APPROVED BY COUNCIL
