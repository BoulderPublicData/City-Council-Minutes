### **A. Opportunity for Council Members and Council Members Elect to voice interest in being nominated for the position of Mayor or Mayor Pro Tem.**

Per Council Procedures in Section IX. – Nomination and Elections:

“On the second Tuesday in November, of every odd-numbered year...council members and council members elect shall gather in council chambers for the purpose of expressing interest in nomination for mayor and/or mayor pro tem for the new council. The mayor shall chair the meeting. Any council member with an unexpired term or council member elect may express his or her interest in serving as mayor or mayor pro tem. Any person expressing an interest shall make a speech regarding his or her qualifications for either or both positions. Each candidate shall make only one speech regardless of whether the council member is seeking either position or both positions. No speech shall exceed five minutes in length.”

**Each nominee was given 5 minutes to speak regarding their qualifications and goals for the office of Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem. The election will commence at the beginning of the meeting on Tuesday, November 17, 2015.**

### **C. CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO ACCEPT THE BOULDER CIVIC AREA, PHASE I PARK DEVELOPMENT PLAN, COMMUNITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT PROCESS (CEAP)**

Parks and Planning Manager, Jeff Haley, Senior Urban Designer, Sam Assefa presented this item to Council.

The Public hearing was opened at 10:38 PM.

1. Leonard Segel, resident and architect, spoke to the preservation of the bandshell as a landmark feature.
2. Kathryn Barth, spoke to the preservation of the bandshell and bandshell seating in addition to the historical importance of its landmark designation. She gave a letter to Council.
3. Abby Daniels, Director of Historic Boulder, spoke in support of permanently preserving the bandshell and removing the seating would weaken an application to the National Registry of Historic Landmarks. She is concerned about the bike path coming into the landmarked boundaries.

{6}------------------------------------------------

4. Karl Anuta, spoke in support of permanently preserving the bandshell and is significant to the history of Boulder. He feels it violates the ordinance and process of the historic landmark process.

There being no further speakers, the public hearing closed at 10:54 PM.

Council asked questions regarding the bandshell seating, relocating, future planning of the Civic Area and proper use of the money that is reserved for remodeling the area. Council discussed the viability of the performance area and the increased traffic going by and the potential events that cannot be held there due to its condition, but at the same time wanted to reactivate the bandshell if possible with better programming and events for the community.

Council discussed the 11<sup>th</sup> Street bridge with mixed views but acknowledged the design and hard work that has gone into the entire Civic Area Plan.

Mayor Appelbaum moved, seconded by Council Member Cowles, to suspend the rules and continue the meeting past 11:00 PM. The motion carried 5:3 with Council Members Karakehian, Plass and Morzel opposed and Council Member Shoemaker absent at 11:02 PM.

Council discussed the possibility of the bandshell going back for reconsideration to the Landmark Board. There was concern about moving it and making an effort to reactivate it where it is.

Council Member Weaver moved, seconded by Council Member Plass, to accept Item 5C with the understanding that the bandshell issue will go back to the Landmarks Board. The motion carried 8:0 with Council Member Shoemaker absent at 11:30 PM.
